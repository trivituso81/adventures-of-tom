## Things the next stage should know

- Composure is a ledger on the action (`c.ledger[foe key] = {n, peeve}`), written by `b3Pips(c,t,n,peeve)` and paid by `b3PayLedger(F,c)`. Every action context must carry `ledger:{}` (b3Act, b3FoeTurn, a reaction, a hazard trip). A status or passive effect that runs inside an action shares that action's ledger (`engine/b3-hooks.js` b3Listen); a reaction has its own.
- `b3PayLedger` can be called more than once for one action (b3Act calls it before the reactions and again after `actionEnd`); `c.paid` keeps the cap across calls. A Trait that adds pips (Deedee's Spotless Floor, Tom's Million-Miler Status, stage 5) should write with `b3Pips` from a listener on `afterHit` or `detonation`, not from `pipsShaved`, or it lands after the first payout and only the second call picks it up.
- Break state on a foe: `pipLock` (no pips come off), `broken` (Flustered, waiting for its turn), `flusterTurn` (the owed turn is running), `flusterMove` (Broken during its own turn: it falls back in `b3EndTurn`). The `flustered` status uses the new duration unit `begin` (ends when the carrier's next turn begins).
- `b3LoseCharge(F,t,A)` is the one place a wound-up act is dropped (interrupt op, Break, a slip): it clears the Mark and counts the act as used.
- A reaction row now carries `root` (the `reacted` record of the action that set it off) and may carry `act` (an enemy counter act) and `tags`. "Once per action" answers cannot be set off twice by answers to answers.
- The enemy act beat's `shape` is `strike` for melee, `throw` for any other act with power, `self` otherwise. A foe act with `then` writes its `telegraph` beat with `g:'post'` and a `mark` key.
- New beats: `pips`, `break`, `summon`; `interrupt` has `why` (and `src:null` when a Break did it); `telegraph` has `mark`; `miss` has why `hidden`.
- `b3Carried` rows now give `left` as the whole phrase ("2 turns left", "until its next turn"). The screen prints it as it is.
- Screen: `B3.view.mark` ({foe key: ally key}), `B3.view.u[key].pips` and `maxPips`. An add has no place on screen until its `summon` beat has played (`B3P.foe[key]` is missing): anything that walks `F.foes` to draw or hit-test must skip those (b3Draw and b3FoeAt do).
- Stage 4 (Vibes): `b3PartyUnit` still takes the first Vibe of `B3_CAST[id].vibes` and applies no Lean. `B3_RULES.leans` and `rankVp` hold the numbers. Rows default from the Lean (`rowByLean`); the Vibes menu is where the player first chooses rows, and Big Glare's `aim:'back'` only matters once someone stands in Back.
- The simple player: `b3Auto(F,u,only)`. The simulator's `--support=4` uses `only` to pick a no-damage move.
- `tools/b3_test.py` stage 3 section tops the boss's pips up at every family turn (`S3_HOOK`, `window.__s3.keep`) so that a Break only happens where the test wants one. A later stage that adds pip sources does not need to change it.
- Spoken lines: still none for any Battle 3 move (`line:null`), 14 moves waiting (architecture risk 8).
