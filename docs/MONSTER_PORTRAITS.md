# Monster dialogue portraits (Tom 2026-11-06, `foeport1`)

Rule: a monster that speaks in a dialogue box gets a portrait, a zoomed-in crop of its face. Silent monsters get none.

Source of the list: every `talk:` speaker in data/scenes*.js and data/bonus-nine-scoops.js, matched to a monster id. Part 2 scenes use short names (HUSH-9, Drill Worm, Forever Engine), mapped by hand. 51 monsters. Files: portraits/foes/<id>.webp. Baker: tools/sprite-bake/bake_foe_portraits.py.

## Not in the list on purpose

- People: Marlowe, Vance, Julian Vance, the Archivist (the Twin Lenses boss is only its eyes). They have boss entries but speak as people, so a boss crop would be the wrong face. They need a painted face of their own.
- Objects and places that talk (Corkboard, The Dome, Turntable, Gate Zero, Whispers, Stillness Core, Dead-Letter Office...) and NPC townsfolk.
- Monsters whose only words are battle lines (Curator, Hushling, Paperweight, Lukewarm, Leftover, Shusher): battle text has no portrait box.
