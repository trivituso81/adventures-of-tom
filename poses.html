Pose Editor

How this works
  1. Pick a person, then Walking, Feelings or Talking, then a pose.
  2. Drag the red line to the top of the head (the skull, not the hair) and the blue line to the bottom of the chin.
  3. Tap LINES ARE RIGHT. The pose is resized by itself so its head and height match the person's standing pose. The picture underneath shows the result.
  4. When you are done, tap SAVE and tell Claude "apply my pose sizes". Nothing changes in the game before that.
