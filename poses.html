Pose Editor

Each pose has three lines: red is the top of the head (the skull, not the hair), blue is the bottom of the chin, white is the ground under the feet. Put the lines where you see them, then AUTO SIZE makes the head and the height right from your lines. Nothing changes in the game until you SAVE and tell Claude "apply my pose sizes".
