DOWNTOWN LA CUTOUT LAYERS: THREE PROMPTS

Why: the game draws the painting flat. To walk BEHIND things, it needs each tall object as its own cutout on a flat magenta background, in exactly the same place as the painting. Send the three prompts one at a time, each with the latest DTLA painting attached (the one with Angel City on the north row and Nickel's on the south row). Three short prompts work better than one long one. Send the three results back with no words and I will install them.

What I do with them: the results only serve as masks. I line each one up with the original painting and cut the objects out of the original, so any small drift or repaint in the generator does not change the look of the game.
