Battle screen redesign

One screen and no menus. Every move the family member has is on screen at once. The first tap on a move shows what it does and what it will do to the target. The second tap does it.

How it compares with modern RPGs

Our system is a turn-order battle with a build-and-spend resource and a break meter. Its closest relatives are Honkai: Star Rail, Octopath Traveler II and Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, with Final Fantasy X's turn order. Their screens share the habits in this table. Today's screen follows two of them in part: Skills show a price, and the turn bar previews a push.
