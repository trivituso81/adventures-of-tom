A fake gamepad works by replacing navigator.getGamepads in an init script. Do not run `pkill -f <pattern>` from the shell tool: the pattern matches the tool's own command line and kills the shell (the same happens with a `pgrep -f` loop whose plain pattern appears in the command; use a regex the command text does not contain literally, e.g. `pgrep -f "bal\.py"`, then `kill` each pid).

## Parts: one game in two halves (decided 2026-10-20)

- The full game is one story split into two Parts, like one game in two halves (not two games). PART 1 is Act I, chapters 1 to 10, ending with chapter 10 Boarding Group Zero. PART 2 is Acts II and III, chapters 11 to 20, opening with the twist (chapter 11 The Layover Between Worlds). The split follows battle-design section 8 (Act I rosters vs. the Boarding Pass Tag-In from chapter 11) and the Tombo / Family Finish split (section 6).
- Title screen: PART 1 and PART 2 both show. PART 2 is greyed out (dashed, shakes when picked) until this device has Part 1 clear data.
- Part 1 clear data: the last scene of chapter 10 (built in C10) must end with `{partCard:1, done:true}` (PART 1 COMPLETE card) and `{partClear:true}`. That step sets flag part1-clear and saves a full snapshot to its own clear-data record (aot-save-clear-1 to 3; the first empty one, else the oldest is replaced; never listed in the save menu) plus the autosave, and toasts that Part 2 is open.
- Starting Part 2: PART 2 lists each clear data (CLEAR DATA n: level, coins, play time, date), plus LOAD for Part 2 saves. Picking one (after the usual autosave confirm) loads it, so everything carries over: every member's level, XP, Stamp Points and stamps, gear, the bag, coins, play time, every story flag (Tombo unlocks, Heart-Traces, the HEART meter, the Kindness scrapbook). - Saves: Part 1 and Part 2 share the autosave and slots 1 to 3. Full-game save summaries start with "Part N · Ch N". LOAD under either Part lists the same saves.
- Rule for chapter sessions: Part 2 content can rely on everything a Part 1 clear save holds, and must not assume anything a player could have skipped. Never reset levels, gear or flags when Part 2 starts.

It calls POST /v1/music (model music_v2_5 by default, music_v2 and music_v1 selectable; music_length_ms; force_instrumental true; mp3_44100_128), three requests at a time at most, retries 429s, keeps one take per track automatically (Redo keeps the new one), has a Loop test button (plays the last ten seconds and fades back into the start) and saves music.json {format:'aot-music-v1', suffix, tracks:[{id,name,group,kind,where,len,dur,prompt,sent,model,bytes,mp3}]}. A suffix is added to every description (loop or jingle wording). The TEST SET is town, battle, victory, defeat; Tom listens first, then presses Make everything left. Cost about 11 to 14 credits per second; full set 1,822 s of music.
- Plan for the game side: merge music.json into data/music/<id>.js (base64 mp3, loaded lazily on demand, never published with voice files, each under 16 MB; ffmpeg trim, loudness-normalize, 96 kbps), engine/music.js plays them with crossfade on area change and on battle start and end (loops are crossfaded in the player; there is no loop option in the API), ducks under dialogue and voice, respects the music toggle and volume slider, unlocks on first tap, and replaces the old synthesized town song (data/songs.js stays as the fallback until all tracks exist). ## Voice and tone rules

- The girls call Tom "Tata" (both syllables stressed equally) in speech only. Every interface, menu and message calls him Tom.
- Tom's exclamations are emphatic ("That was UNCALLED for!").
- Dialogue is short, warm and funny. Jokes come from character, not randomness. No em dashes anywhere in game text.

## Voice Baker

- Tom makes clips with the Voice Baker (rebuilt 2026-10-19 as voice-baker.html), a standalone HTML file he opens in desktop Chrome (published artifacts cannot reach ElevenLabs; Chrome is needed for the mic). It needs his ElevenLabs API key (kept in that browser), lists every line per character with its status and the clip currently in the game, and saves voices.json.
- Source lives in the project: claude/voice-baker-template.html plus claude/voice-baker-build.js. To rebuild: read data/lines.js and data/voice-clips.js from the artifact, then run `node voice-baker-build.js <folder with both files>` next to the template (it embeds LINES, the baked clips themselves so Tom can compare old vs new, and the saved voices) and send Tom voice-baker.html. Rebuild whenever lines change.
- How it directs delivery (reason for the 2026-10-19 rebuild: the old clips were made on eleven_multilingual_v2, which ignores bracketed directions):
  - Model per character, default eleven_v4 (newest, most expressive, takes [audio tags]); eleven_v3 and eleven_multilingual_v2 still selectable. v4 and v3 get only stability and similarity; style and speaker boost are sent only for v2.
  - Stability presets Creative 0 / Natural 0.5 / Robust 1; similarity slider.
  - Each line has a Script (what ElevenLabs reads: [tags], CAPS, ellipses, respellings) separate from the game text it is saved under, so directions never break say()'s exact-text match. Scripts start as a per-kind default tag plus the line (defaultTag in the template). An optional character direction is merged into each line's first bracket.
  - Generate 3 takes per line (random seeds); takes persist in that browser (IndexedDB); Tom ticks the ones to keep.
  - Record mode: Tom records the line himself; speech-to-speech (POST /v1/speech-to-speech/{voice_id}, multipart: audio, model_id eleven_english_sts_v2 or eleven_multilingual_sts_v2, voice_settings JSON, remove_background_noise) turns it into the character's voice, keeping his timing and emphasis.
- Nara is a dog, so her lines use ElevenLabs Sound Effects (POST /v1/sound-generation, model eleven_text_to_sound_v2, body {text, duration_seconds, prompt_influence}) from an editable description and length per line (DOG defaults; bark counts added from the line's "Arf"s); 3 takes per line. Saved clips use the line's text, so say('nara',kind) plays them like voice clips.
- voices.json = {clips:{character:{kind:[{text,mp3}]}}, voices}. clips holds only the newly kept takes. voices[ch] = {voice_id, name, model, settings, direction, sts_model, scripts:{"kind|text": script}}; voices.nara = {sfx:true, model, prompt_influence, prompts:{"kind|text":{p,d}}}. Scripts and prompts come back into the baker on the next rebuild.
- When Tom sends voices.json: merge its clips into data/voice-clips.js (main build only; demo/ stays frozen unless Tom asks). For each line with kept takes, the new takes replace all old clips with the same text (every character, not just Nara); then replace the saved voices with the new voices block. Test that the clips play and publish.

- Voice files are split: data/voice-clips.js holds voices and Nara; each family member's battle clips are in data/voice/<id>.js; dialogue clips per chapter in data/voice/dialog-chN.js keyed "voice|exact text". Merge tools: claude/voice-merge.js (full), claude/merge-one.js (one character), claude/merge-tombo.js (holds clips baked in a stale voice id). Dialogue baking: claude/voice-baking.md.

## Battle rules (as built through F7 and C2; the design is claude/battle-design.md)

- Party of 1 to 4 on a CTB turn ribbon. Turn order: ticks = floor(1200/(PEP+30) x weight) with weights quick .7, standard 1, heavy 1.3 (and instant .01, only for Tom's Diamond Medallion); the lowest count acts, party first on ties; the opening order gives the party a small head start. Statuses that change PEP change the ribbon.
- VP: cap 10, start 5 (plus any startVp perk), +2 at the start of each own turn, +1 on the basic move. Basics are free; path skills cost 2 to 6; signatures cost 1 to 4.
- Six stats (HP, POW, GRIT, WARMTH, COOL, PEP) from level 1 / level 50 lines per character (B2_STATS) at the member's level (1 to 50), plus flat gear stats (Tool, Outfit), times percent perks (charms, passive stamps) and statuses in battle. Damage = max(1, (POW x 2.5 - GRIT x 1.2) x mult) for POW moves, WARMTH vs COOL for warmth moves, the average for hybrid moves; then element x1.5 (weak) or x0.5 (every element resisted), crit x1.5 (5% plus the move's, statuses' and perks' crit; Grace's crits also take her Trending multiplier; a move or Tombo with crit 1 always crits), skill boost perks (Study Streak, Cone Six: +20% on named skills), Pumped Up x1.5 (used up by the next attack or heal), debuff bonus (Kanji Strike), damage-taken statuses (Guard -50%, Nobody Gets Past -40%, Staggered +50%), Tom guarding himself -40%, and +/-8% spread. A landed High-Five adds x1.15 to whatever has not landed yet. Heal = a share of the target's max HP plus the healer's WARMTH x the move's factor (Pumped Up x1.5; Grace's heals x her Trending multiplier; High-Five x1.15). Rose Mode takes 40% off every hit on the party; Director Mode gives monsters 25% more POW, WARMTH and PEP.
- Six elements: Kinetic, Cozy, Splash, Sparkle, Chatter, Amber. Weaknesses show after a reveal (Flashcard Flurry, Red Pen, or landing a weak hit) in the target list, in Inspect and as a WEAK: line over the enemy.
- Targets: one enemy, splash (60% to row neighbors), a row, the front row, all, multi (random hits), one ally, a knocked-out or living ally (WARDROBE), the party, self. Formations: up to 3 front and 2 back, 4 in all; duplicate names get letters (Mudling A, B). Cast Line pulls a back-row enemy to the front; Piggyback Pounce knocks one to the back.
- Statuses (B2_STATUS): Bodyguarded and Guarding (DEFEND), Guard (-50% taken), Pumped Up, Tucked In (15% regen a turn, GRIT and COOL +25%), Wardrobe Fresh (one auto-revive at 50%, blocks debuffs), Nobody Gets Past (-40% taken), POW / GRIT / PEP / WARMTH up, Kiln Glow (WARMTH and COOL +25%, from Glaze & Bake), Personal Best, Glitter Nails (crit +25%, and 20% of WARMTH damage taken bounces back at the attacker), Genre Costume, TNB's rack costumes (Action Hero: POW and GRIT +25%; Cozy Cardigan: WARMTH and COOL +25%; Final Girl: gets back up once at 50%), Shoulder Ride (shown as Rose's badge, not a tag), Evasive (single hits miss half the time), Spotlight (Grace draws hits aimed at allies under half HP), Pie Tin; debuffs Goofed, Smitten, Staggered and Seeing Stars (stunned, from a Tombo or Boppy's Counter-Roar) (each loses its next turn), Grumbled (POW and WARMTH -30% and it goes after Boppy), Shushed (no Chatter skills, no Tombos), Amber-Stuck (PEP -50%), Slowed, Drowsy (PEP -30%; also from the Big Shake's Smog Puff), Scared Stiff, Dizzy (its single attacks miss 25%), GRIT / COOL / POW down, Hooked (cannot dodge). Durations count down at the start of the owner's turns. A heavy physical hit snaps an enemy out of Goofed. Bosses resist Goofed and Smitten more than half the time unless goofable. Charm immunities block a status (IMMUNE pops up). Enemy acts can carry a status (`status:[id,turns]` on the act).
- KO: at 0 HP a member drops out of the turn order (KO pose, KNOCKED OUT on their card) and loses their statuses; WARDROBE or the Wake-Up Whistle brings them back. Wardrobe Fresh, then Final Girl, catch a knockout first. Under 25% HP a member kneels with a black eye (Nara sits). In Rose Mode, when the whole party is down, Deedee's emergency Rhubarb Pie revives everyone at 50% once a fight.
- DEFEND: Tom guards an ally (or himself) for 2 of his turns; single hits aimed at that ally go to Tom at 40% less.
- Traits (F4b, 2026-10-20; engine/battle2-traits.js; numbers in B2_TRAIT and B2_RACK). Each shows as a badge along the bottom of the member's portrait on the party card; a ready trait glows, and the command hint mentions it. The menu's STATUS page explains each one (B6_TRAITS).
  - Tom, Diamond Medallion: DEFEND on an ally (not himself) or any Tombo earns 1 Upgrade Mile, max 3 (badge ◇◇◇ fills to ◆◆◆). At 3 Miles his next path skill is FREE (0 VP) and instant: he goes again right away. The Miles are spent when he uses it.
  - Grace, Trending Meter: each move different from her last one raises it a step (x1.0, x1.2, x1.5; TRENDING pops); the same move twice in a row resets it (OLD NEWS). It multiplies her heals and her critical hits. Badge x1.0 to x1.5.
  - Rose, Shoulder Ride: Lamby Launch, Piggyback Pounce and Double-Decker Boop end with Rose up on Tom's shoulders (she says her baked "I am the tallest! Giddyup, TAta!"). She rides for 3 of her turns (badge RIDE n), drawn sitting on Tom; it ends early if Tom is knocked out. While riding, single hits aimed at her go to Tom at 40% less, and her path skills hit a second time from up top at 40% (DOUBLE-DECKER!). She hops down to do each move and climbs back after.
  - Amy, Pace Setter: every action Amy takes adds a Cadence stack (+5% PEP for the whole party, max 5 = +25%; badge PACE n). A knockout resets it.
  - Deedee, Warm Crust Tokens: Rhubarb Crisp leaves 1 crust on the shared tray (even when fed to a monster), Sunday Dinner fills it (3), Sourdough & Rhubarb adds 2; max 3. Nara eats them too (homemade): 40% and POW +30%.

## Progression (F6, 2026-10-20; data/progress.js, engine/progress.js; battle-design sections 5 and 7)

- Levels 1 to 50, a flat 100 XP each (member.level, member.xp toward the next level). Max HP and the six stats follow B2_STATS by level, plus gear. A level up refills HP. Tom's title still changes at levels 3 (Regular) and 5 (Local Busybody).
- Battle XP (b6BattleXp): each monster in the fight gives its B6_XP value (Mudling 12, Skeeter 24, Pond Grump 26, Bog Toad 30, Log Lump 34; Smoglet 13, Ticket Flurry 26, Canyon Coyote 28, Gridlock 32, Tar Pit Blob 35; Soggy Sock 15, Bobber 27, Tangle 30, Snag 36; others 10 + 3 x level), +5% for each time that map monster has been beaten (max +50%). A fight with a boss gives 100 (one full level). Everyone in the party gets the full amount, knocked out or not.
- Overlevel instant win (F7, EarthBound style): when Tom is 5 or more levels above a non-boss map monster (its level is the larger of its MONSTERS and B2_FOES level, plus its tier), touching it (or pressing A on it) skips the battle: a confetti POP and "+N XP" over it on the map, a toast with the XP, coins, any drop and level ups, the same XP, coins (its formation's purse plus charm coin perks) and drop chance as a won fight, its tier goes up and it rests 25 seconds, then an autosave.
- The Family Group Chat (shared story XP, b6Sync): after every win, every member, home or away, is raised to Tom's total XP (never lowered). A recruit who caught up while at home says "Caught up to Lv n" when they join. Members keep their own stamps and gear.
- Stamp Points: 1 to start, +1 at every 3rd level (3, 6, 9...), plus member.spBonus (1 per finished side quest; none awarded yet). That is 14 by level 39 and 17 at level 50: one full path (10) plus a few nodes of the other. The victory card shows +SP next to a level up that gives a point and ends with "★ New Stamp Points! - Stampcards (B6_PATHS): every character has two paths of 4 nodes, costing 1, 2, 3 and 4 SP, stamped in order, each with a level gate (the battle-design levels).
