# Chapter 1: The Big One

Status: LOCKED by Tom 2026-11-16 19:35 ("Lock"). This replaces the chapter 1 that was locked on 2026-11-13. It began as a 70-line script Tom workshopped with Gemini, and was revised here over three rounds of his line-by-line marks (22 marks, then 11, then 1). How it was written and what his marks teach: claude/story-writing-method.md.

What happens, in plain words: Tom lands for a weekend on Long Island. The girls run up to him in the Square and walk him to Deedee's for apple muffins. A ball of mud barges into the kitchen, knocks Tom and the girls over and runs off with Deedee's good muffin pan. Tom, Grace, Rose and Deedee chase it to the pond. At Boppy's fishing spot they find a hand-sized patch of grey grass, an orange cube the size of a plum with a dragonfly frozen inside, and a burnt flashbulb. Across the pond, the fish Boppy has talked about for twenty years is now the size of a cab and wearing the pan as a crown. Rose sneaks up on it and says BOO, and it laughs. It gives back the pan and gives Rose a gold coin with a dragonfly on it. On Sunday Tom leaves for the airport, and TNB texts about purple smog over 2nd Avenue.

What the family knows at the end: something made the pond things huge, bright and able to talk, and the fish says there was a flash. Nobody connects the coin to the cube. Rose has the coin. Deedee has the bulb in her kitchen drawer.

Line marks: (GAME) means the line is in the live game word for word, so its recorded clip still plays. Every other line is new and needs recording.

## Scene 1: The Square

[Cab 7 pulls up at the Square. Tom climbs out with his weekend bag and stretches.]
- (GAME) Tom: Oof, I'm finally here! At least the 6 hours went by fast on Delta One! ;)

[Grace and Rose run across the Square at him.]
- (GAME) Grace: TATA! You're BACK!
- (GAME) Grace: We waited for you for a hundred years.
- (GAME) Rose: A hundred MILLION years!
- (GAME) Rose: Tata, up! Up up up!

[Tom lifts Rose onto his shoulders.]
- (GAME) Rose: I'm the tallest! Giddyup, Tata!
- (GAME) Tom: Hello, Rose. Please keep your arms and legs inside the uncle at all times.

[Grace holds up a glittering gold squishy.]
- Grace: Look what I got. Sparkling Diamond Gold Dumpling Squishy. They only made two in the whole world.
- Tom: Where's the other one? A museum?
- Grace: Nobody knows. Come on, Deedee made apple muffins.

## Scene 2: Passing Amy's house

[Rivituso Row, outside the house marked GREGORI. Tom's phone buzzes.]
- Amy (text): Saw you three walk past my window, Brother! Did the girls tackle you at Sal's?
- Tom (text): Rose is steering. Send help.
- Amy (text): Go see Mom and Dad. I'll come by soon!

## Scene 3: Deedee's kitchen

[A pan of apple muffins cools by the back screen door.]
- Deedee: My favorite son! You must be starving!
- Tom: I had two breakfasts, Mom. Yes, I'm starving.

[Tom sets his bag down by the pantry door frame. Two new pencil marks sit above the last GRACE line he remembers. He looks at them for a moment. Boppy is at the counter with his back to the door, holding up a small metal ring from the hardware store.]
- Boppy: Tom! Look at this washer. Brand new, and it bent right in my fingers. They make metal out of chewing gum these days!

[BANG. A ball of mud rolls in, knocks Tom, Grace and Rose flat, grabs the muffin pan and waddles out. Rose scrambles behind Tom's legs.]
- Rose: AHHH! DEEDEE!
- Boppy: Why are you three on the floor?
- Deedee: A ball of mud just stole my good muffin tin!
- Boppy: That wasn't Rose?
- Rose: I'm not MUD, Boppy!

[Through the screen door, the mud ball bounces toward the pond.]
- Deedee: Shoes, everybody. I want my tin back.

[Deedee, Tom and the girls run out the back door. Boppy goes back to his washer.]

## Scene 4: The path into Grump Hollow

[Three mud monsters pop out of the reeds with crumbs on their chins.]
- Grace: There's MORE of them?
- Tom: And they're eating the evidence.
- Deedee: That's my cinnamon topping!
- Rose: Give back Deedee's muffins!

Fight.

## Scene 5: Boppy's fishing spot

[Beside Boppy's folding chair, a hand-sized patch of grass is flat grey. On it sits an orange cube the size of a plum, with a dragonfly frozen inside. One side of the cube has a small round dent.]
- Rose: A marble! With a bug in it!
- Grace: That's a dragonfly. It's frozen inside an orange cube.
- Deedee: And look at the grass under it. Grey as dishwater.

[Rose pulls at it. It does not move.]
- Rose: It's STUCK! Tata, you pull it!

[Tom pulls with both hands. Nothing.]
- Tom: Nope. That is not moving.

[Deedee picks a burnt flashbulb out of the grass with a tissue.]
- Deedee: And somebody left a burnt flashbulb. Now they're littering at my pond.
- Grace: I'm starting a list. Grey grass, burnt bulb, bug in an orange cube. In pencil, until I'm sure.
- Rose: That's Boppy's chair! That's where the Big One lives!

[A huge splash from across the pond.]

## Scene 6: The Big One

[A bright green fish the size of a cab sits half out of the water, wearing the muffin pan as a crown. Something gold shines in its lip.]
- Tom: That fish is bigger than Sal's cab.
- Grace: Boppy was RIGHT?
- Deedee: Nobody tell him.
- The Big One: GLUB. This morning I was a fish. Then there was a big flash. Now I'm a BIG fish. With a crown.
- Deedee: That's not a crown, mister. That's my good twelve-cup muffin pan.

Fight. [Then Rose tiptoes around behind the fish.]
- Rose: BOO!

[The fish jumps. The pan flies off its head. Then it laughs so hard the whole pond shakes.]
- The Big One: GLUB! Nobody ever snuck up on me before!
- Tom: She does that.

[The fish nudges the pan back across the water, then works a gold coin out of its lip and holds it out to Rose. The coin has a dragonfly stamped on it.]
- The Big One: Take your pan. And this is for the small scary one. It's my shiniest thing.
- Rose: It has a bug on it! Thank you, Mr. Fish!
- The Big One: The old man with the hat. Is he coming tomorrow?
- Grace: Boppy? He comes every day.
- The Big One: Tell him to bring better bait.

[The fish sinks. Deedee looks into the pan. It is empty.]
- Deedee: Twelve muffins, gone. I'm a little flattered.

## Scene 7: Home, then Sunday

[Deedee tucks the flashbulb into the back of the kitchen drawer. Rose runs to Boppy with her coin.]
- Rose: Boppy! The Big One is REAL! He gave me this!
- Boppy: He's REAL? Twenty years I've been telling everybody!

[Boppy takes the coin and holds it up to the light.]
- Boppy: That's real gold, Cozy Rosie. Gold like my teeth. Look.

[He grins and taps a back tooth. His fillings shine the same gold.]
- Rose: Boppy has treasure in his mouth!
- Boppy: Put it somewhere safe. Gold is for keeping.

[Sunday. Tom picks up his bag at the front door. Rose hugs his waist.]
- Rose: Don't go back on the airplane, Tata.
- Tom: I'll be back soon, Cozy Rosie. Guard that coin.
- Grace: Text me when you land. I'm keeping the list.

[Tom rides off in Cab 7. His phone buzzes.]
- TNB (text): Well, well, well... can't wait to see you, babe. Weird purple smog sitting over 2nd Ave today.
- Tom: ...Purple smog?

[The cab pulls away toward the airport.]
