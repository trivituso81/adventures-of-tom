## 01. Fact Check (Basic (free), 0 MP)
- What it does in battle: One chatter hit on one enemy.
- Story beat: Olivia flips open a reporter's notebook and clicks her pen. She squints at the enemy with one eyebrow raised, then leans in and jabs the pen at it like a microphone, shouting for its source.
- Spoken lines: "Quick question." (frame 1) / "SOURCE?" (frame 3)
- Facing: LEFT in every frame.
- Frame keys: fc_open, fc_squint, fc_jab, fc_smug

1. **OPEN**: standing, feet shoulder width apart, the notebook held open in her left hand at chest height, her right thumb clicking the pen, a bright professional smile, looking at the enemy on the left.
2. **SQUINT**: leaning forward a little, one eyebrow raised high and the other eye squinted in suspicion, pen held up beside her cheek, notebook still open.
3. **JAB** (HIT): THIS IS THE FRAME THE MOVE LANDS ON. A big lunge to the left, right arm fully extended with the pen pointed forward like a microphone, mouth wide open shouting, the notebook pulled in tight against her ribs, hair swinging back.
4. **SMUG**: straightening up, pen lowered and tucked behind her ear, notebook open in her left hand, one eyebrow raised, a satisfied half smile.

## 02. SNACK BAG (Signature (special), 0 MP)
- What it does in battle: Party heal 25 percent, cures Dizzy, adds a Heart.
- Story beat: Olivia digs through her overstuffed mom tote, pulls out a bright snack pouch in triumph, then tosses one to every friend beside her with a sideways underhand flick, finishing with a stern finger raised: one each.
- Spoken lines: "Hang on, I've got snacks." (frame 1) / "Everybody gets one. ONE." (frame 5)
- Facing: LEFT in every frame.
- Frame keys: sb_dig, sb_found, sb_toss_wind, sb_toss, sb_one_each

1. **DIG**: bent forward at the waist, tote hanging from her left forearm, her right arm elbow-deep inside the bag, head tilted down and to the side with a focused searching frown, hair falling forward.
2. **FOUND**: standing tall, right arm raised high holding up the pink snack pouch in triumph, tote at her hip, wide delighted grin, eyes on the pouch.
3. **TOSS WIND**: her right arm swung back low behind her hip holding the pouch, knees bent, torso twisted away, eyes locked forward with a playful smirk.
4. **TOSS** (HIT): THIS IS THE FRAME THE MOVE LANDS ON. Underhand follow-through, right arm swung forward and out to the left at hip height with the hand open and empty (the pouch has been released and is not drawn), one foot stepping forward, big warm smile.
5. **ONE EACH**: standing with the tote at her hip, right index finger raised in a stern "one" gesture, eyebrows lifted, a teasing smile.

## 03. LivNews Live (Ability 1, 2 MP)
- What it does in battle: Light chatter hit on all enemies.
- Story beat: Olivia holds her phone up selfie-style, fixes her hair, then swings the phone around to point at the enemies and reports live to her followers. She finishes by tapping furiously as the notifications roll in.
- Spoken lines: "LivNews, we're live." (frame 3) / "Chat, look at this thing." (frame 4)
- Facing: LEFT in every frame.
- Frame keys: ln_selfie, ln_turn, ln_live, ln_tapping, ln_check

1. **SELFIE**: phone held up at arm's length in her right hand in front of her face, her left hand smoothing her hair, head tilted, a dazzling TV-reporter smile.
2. **TURN**: swinging the phone around to point it at the enemy on the left, her body twisted toward the left, eyes narrowed with news-anchor focus, left hand pointing a finger flat in front of her chest.
3. **LIVE** (HIT): THIS IS THE FRAME THE MOVE LANDS ON. Standing tall, phone raised high and pointed left, left hand gesturing broadly, mouth open mid-sentence reporting, eyebrows up, full broadcast energy.
4. **TAPPING**: phone held close in both hands at chest height, both thumbs tapping, head tilted down at the screen, a delighted open-mouth grin, shoulders bouncing.
5. **CHECK**: phone lowered to her chest in one hand, looking at the screen with a smug satisfied smile, one eyebrow raised.

## 04. Inverted Pyramid (Ability 2, 3 MP)
- What it does in battle: One kinetic hit, lowers GRIT.
- Story beat: Olivia becomes a journalism professor. She lectures, sketches the inverted pyramid in the air (wide at the top, narrowing to a point), then slams the point down on the enemy.
- Spoken lines: "Most important thing FIRST." (frame 1) / "That's the lede. Bury that." (frame 5)
- Facing: LEFT in every frame.
- Frame keys: ip_lecture, ip_top_edge, ip_slope, ip_point, ip_slam, ip_nod

1. **LECTURE**: professor stance, one index finger raised high, the other hand tucked under her elbow, chin lifted, a knowing teacherly smile, mouth open mid-lecture.
2. **TOP EDGE**: right arm swept wide across her body to the left at shoulder height, finger out like drawing a long line in the air, eyes following her hand, serious expression.
3. **SLOPE**: right arm angled steeply down and to the left, fingers pointing toward the ground, left hand now raised to mirror the first line, eyes narrowed, tongue-in-cheek concentration.
4. **POINT**: both hands pressed together, fingers pointing straight down in a sharp V point at waist height, elbows out, head tilted, eyebrows raised in dramatic anticipation.
5. **SLAM** (HIT): THIS IS THE FRAME THE MOVE LANDS ON. A big lunge to the left, both pressed hands thrust downward and forward in a stabbing point, her mouth wide open shouting, hair flying, the whole body behind the blow.
6. **NOD**: standing upright, arms crossed, chin lifted, eyes closed, a satisfied teacher's nod.

## 05. Double Under (Ability 3, 4 MP)
- What it does in battle: Three kinetic hits on one enemy. The first does 0 (rusty).
- Story beat: Olivia pulls out a jump rope and starts slow. She trips on the first jump, laughs it off, gets competitive, then the teen jump rope champion comes back: a flurry of double-unders ending in a whip of the rope at the enemy.
- Spoken lines: "I was a champion, you know." (frame 1) / "Okay, that was a while ago." (frame 3) / "There it is!" (frame 6)
- Facing: LEFT in every frame.
- Frame keys: du_ready, du_trip, du_laugh, du_focus, du_jump_a, du_jump_b, du_whip

1. **READY**: standing, a rope handle in each hand held low at her hips, the rope hanging in a slack curve behind her heels, a cocky confident smile, chin up.
2. **TRIP**: half-jumping with both feet tangled in the rope at her ankles, arms flailing, wincing mouth in an "oops", eyes wide, off-balance.
3. **LAUGH**: rope hanging from one hand, her other hand pushing her hair back, a sheepish embarrassed laugh, eyes squeezed, shoulders shrugged.
4. **FOCUS**: crouched slightly with the handles at her hips and the rope behind her, jaw set, narrowed determined eyes, a competitive smirk.
5. **JUMP A**: high in the air, both feet off the ground and tucked, the rope in a clear circle passing under her feet, wrists flicked out, arms low and tight, a huge joyful grin, hair lifting.
6. **JUMP B**: at the very top of a higher jump, knees pulled up, the rope in a bigger arc overhead, hair flying, mouth open in a shout of triumph.
7. **WHIP** (HIT): THIS IS THE FRAME THE MOVE LANDS ON. Landing in a lunge to the left, right arm whipped fully forward with the handle at the end of a long straight rope pointing at the enemy, left hand pulled back, a fierce champion smile.

## 06. Story Time (Ability 4, 4 MP)
- What it does in battle: Cozy kind move: the enemy loses its next turn, plus warmth and a Heart.
- Story beat: Olivia sits cross-legged, opens her own children's book and reads the first page in a soft storyteller voice until the enemy nods off.
- Spoken lines: "Once upon a time, in a very quiet house..." (frame 3) / "And everybody went to sleep." (frame 5)
- Facing: LEFT in every frame.
- Frame keys: st_sit, st_show, st_read, st_shhh, st_close

1. **SIT**: sitting cross-legged on the ground, the book resting open on her lap, both hands on the pages, her skirt tucked neatly over her knees, a gentle soft smile, looking down at the book.
2. **SHOW**: sitting cross-legged, the book held up open and turned outward toward the enemy on the left, one hand raised flat to present the page, a warm inviting storyteller face.
3. **READ** (HIT): THIS IS THE FRAME THE MOVE LANDS ON. Sitting cross-legged, the book held in front of her at chest height, one finger tracing a line, eyes down on the page, her mouth open mid-word, a soothing relaxed expression.
4. **SHHH**: sitting cross-legged, the book pressed to her chest in one arm, her other index finger across her lips, eyes wide and loving, a very quiet smile.
5. **CLOSE**: sitting cross-legged, the book closed in both hands against her chest, eyes closed, a deeply serene smile, head tilted slightly.

## 07. MICHAEL! (Ability 5, 5 MP)
- What it does in battle: Big kinetic hit, lowers PEP. Michael is never seen; the game flies a diaper bag in.
- Story beat: Olivia cups her hands and yells for her husband Michael. Nothing. She yells again, louder. A diaper bag flies in from off screen on the right (the game draws it) and flattens the enemy. She thanks him. Michael is never drawn.
- Spoken lines: "MICHAEL!" (frame 2) / "MICHAEL, HANDLE THIS!" (frame 4) / "Thank you, honey." (frame 5)
- Facing: Frame 1 faces LEFT. Frames 2, 3, 4 and 5 face RIGHT (she is calling toward the right edge of the screen).
- Frame keys: mi_breath, mi_yell_1, mi_wait, mi_yell_2, mi_thanks

1. **BREATH**: facing LEFT, standing tall, taking a huge breath, shoulders lifted, hands coming up to cup around her mouth, eyes wide with mom determination.
2. **YELL 1**: facing RIGHT, both hands cupped around her mouth, head tipped back and turned up to the right, mouth wide open in a big shout, hair swinging.
3. **WAIT**: facing RIGHT, one hand cupped behind her ear, the other hand on her hip, head tilted, a flat annoyed "I am waiting" look with one eyebrow raised.
4. **YELL 2** (HIT): THIS IS THE FRAME THE MOVE LANDS ON. Facing RIGHT, leaning far forward with her feet planted wide, cupped hands at her mouth, one arm flung out pointing to the right, face red with exasperation, mouth wide open yelling, hair flying.
5. **THANKS**: facing RIGHT, a hand on her hip, the other hand giving a small satisfied wave to the right, a warm sweet smile, eyes closed in relief.

## 08. Better Than Bobby (Ultimate (path skill), 8 MP)
- What it does in battle: Five rising chatter and sparkle hits spread over all enemies. The five count-off frames are five hits.
- Story beat: Olivia counts on her fingers everything she beats her brother Bobby at: jump rope, grades, parallel parking, cooking, Mario Kart. Each count is a hit. She finishes with a mic drop.
- Spoken lines: "Jump rope. Grades. Parallel parking." (frames 1 to 3) / "Cooking. Mario Kart." (frames 4 and 5) / "Sorry, Bobby." (frame 7)
- Facing: LEFT in every frame.
- Frame keys: bb_one, bb_two, bb_three, bb_four_and_five, bb_crossed, bb_mic, bb_drop

1. **ONE**: standing, one index finger held up in front of her at chest height, chin lifted, smug raised eyebrow, mouth open mid-word, hand toward the enemy on the left.
2. **TWO**: two fingers held up, head tilted to one side, a smug closed-mouth smile, free hand tucked on her hip.
3. **THREE**: three fingers held up, her body leaning back with a proud sway, eyes half closed, an "obviously" expression.
4. **FOUR AND FIVE**: a whole hand held up with all five fingers spread and the other hand pointing at it, leaning forward slightly, eyes bright, a huge competitive grin.
5. **CROSSED**: arms crossed, chin high, nose in the air, a triumphant smirk, hair tossed over one shoulder.
6. **MIC**: right arm extended out to the left holding the small microphone out at arm's length, left hand on her hip, a big victorious grin, knees slightly bent.
7. **DROP** (HIT): THIS IS THE FRAME THE MOVE LANDS ON. Right arm still extended to the left but the hand open and empty (the microphone is gone), head tilted, one eyebrow raised, an utterly smug smile, body leaning back in a casual victory pose.

## 09. Is This Real? (Path skill B1, 3 MP)
- What it does in battle: One chatter hit, inflicts Dizzy.
- Story beat: Olivia holds her phone up to the enemy and scans it, squinting. She announces it has too many fingers and is a fake, leaving the enemy confused.
- Spoken lines: "Hold still, I'm checking." (frame 1) / "Too many fingers. Fake." (frame 4)
- Facing: LEFT in every frame.
- Frame keys: ir_scan, ir_squint, ir_gasp, ir_fake, ir_sigh

1. **SCAN**: standing, right arm stretched out to the left holding the phone up toward the enemy like a scanner, her left hand held up in a "hold still" palm, a focused professional expression.
2. **SQUINT**: leaning in close with the phone held steady in front of her, one eye squinted and the other wide, head tilted, a suspicious frown.
3. **GASP**: phone lowered to chest height, eyes wide and darting from the phone to the enemy, mouth open in a realization, one eyebrow high.
4. **FAKE** (HIT): THIS IS THE FRAME THE MOVE LANDS ON. Head shaking, the phone held at her side, her free hand pointing a finger straight at the enemy, mouth open in a firm scolding "fake", a disappointed journalist glare.
5. **SIGH**: standing, phone lowered against her hip, free hand on her forehead, eyes closed in a tired "I have seen this before" sigh.

## 10. Marine Layer (Path skill B2, 4 MP)
- What it does in battle: Splash hit on all enemies, lowers COOL and PEP.
- Story beat: Olivia, a native Southern Californian, is freezing at sixty-two degrees. She pulls a hoodie on over her dress, hunches in misery, and dramatically declares a crisis as gray coastal fog rolls over the enemies (the game draws the fog).
- Spoken lines: "It's sixty-two degrees." (frame 3) / "This is a crisis." (frame 4)
- Facing: LEFT in every frame.
- Frame keys: ml_shiver, ml_hoodie, ml_hood_up, ml_crisis, ml_sulk

1. **SHIVER**: in her fruit-print dress only, arms wrapped tightly around herself, shoulders hunched up to her ears, knees knocking together, eyes squeezed, lips pressed in a shiver.
2. **HOODIE**: pulling the gray hoodie on, both arms halfway into the sleeves and raised above her head, her head poking out of the neck hole, the hood bunched behind her neck, a relieved wince.
3. **HOOD UP**: the hoodie on with the hood up over her hair, both hands deep in the front pouch, hunched and miserable, her mouth open in a complaint, the dress hem showing below the hoodie.
4. **CRISIS** (HIT): THIS IS THE FRAME THE MOVE LANDS ON. The hoodie on with the hood down, one hand pressed dramatically to her forehead, the other arm flung out to the side, head thrown back, mouth open in a theatrical wail, eyes squeezed shut.
5. **SULK**: the hoodie on with the hood up, arms crossed over her chest, shoulders up, eyes rolling to the side, a deadpan frown.

## 11. LACHLAN, NO! (Path skill B3, 5 MP)
- What it does in battle: Big kinetic hit, cancels the enemy's wind-up.
- Story beat: Olivia spots her four-year-old son Lachlan (off screen, never drawn) about to climb something dangerous. She sprints straight through the enemy at full mom speed, then skids to a stop with her arms out catching him, and sighs with relief. No child is ever drawn.
- Spoken lines: "LACHLAN, NO!" (frame 1) / "Got him. Got him. We're fine." (frame 5)

- Frame keys: lk_gasp, lk_sprint_wind, lk_sprint, lk_skid, lk_relief

2. **SPRINT WIND**: a sprinter's crouch, leaning far forward, weight on the back leg, both fists clenched at her sides, jaw clenched, eyes locked on the left, hair swept back.
3. **SPRINT** (HIT): THIS IS THE FRAME THE MOVE LANDS ON. Full stride to the left, mid-air with both feet off the ground, arms pumping, body leaning almost horizontal, mouth open in a roar, skirt and hair streaming behind.

5. **RELIEF**: standing upright, both arms curled in front of her chest as if holding something small close (nothing is drawn in them), eyes closed, a shaky relieved smile, head bowed.
