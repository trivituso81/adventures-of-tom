## Problem 1: the button stays ASKED, WAITING (cause found, NOT fixed by -6)

engine/art-clips.js, clipPoll: on a matching report it sets MSG[id] and calls refreshItem(id) BEFORE `clipAskedSet(left)` removes the press from localStorage. clipCard reads `clipAsked(e.id)` from localStorage, so it redraws "ASKED, WAITING / Waiting for GitHub." under "Made and approved". Then the list is cleared with no redraw and no further poll. Stuck until reload or 3 hours. fix-keysecrets2-try-6 (cd77917) only saved the last message to the device; the ordering bug is still on main.

Fix: `clipAskedSet(left)` before the refreshItem calls (or refresh every finished id after it), and re-read the entry after an approval so the version list updates.

## Problem 2: no way to review a GitHub-made video

The card's REVIEW buttons come from RES (videos made on this device). Videos made by the clip-gen Action never appear there, and the job approves them itself, so "Approved" means the gate approved, not Tom. The only place to see them is Battle Lab > CHAPTER 1 REVIEW, which the card never says.

Decision (Claude, 16:40): a single GENERATE VIDEO press is made and checked by the gate but NOT approved; it waits for Tom. GENERATE CHAPTER keeps auto-approve (Tom asked for that on 11-18: "can't we just do all of chapter 1 at once").
