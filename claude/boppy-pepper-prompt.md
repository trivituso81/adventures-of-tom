## Prompt text

IMAGE REQUEST: ONE SINGLE CHARACTER ANIMATION SHEET. Return exactly one image, nothing else.

WHAT THIS IS
Animation frames for "The Adventures of Tom", a family role-playing game in the style of a 16-bit Super Nintendo game (Final Fantasy VI, Chrono Trigger). The character is Boppy, a grumpy but lovable grandfather. The attached reference image shows Boppy. Draw him EXACTLY like the reference in every frame: same face, mustache, cap, clothes, colors, proportions, art style and level of detail. His baseball cap stays on his head in every frame.

THE MOVE
"Pepper Breath": Boppy walks up holding a pepper he grew in his own backyard, shows it off proudly, bites it, chews, and his whole face turns bright red. He fans his face, takes a huge breath, and breathes out as hard as he can, like a dragon. Then he collapses into a gasp, grumbles about his stomach, and recovers with a smug look. Funny and warm, never mean.

THE SHEET (strict)
- A grid of 4 columns by 4 rows of square cells, 256 x 256 pixels each, so the full image is 1024 x 1024. If your tool offers a different size, keep exactly 4 columns and 4 rows.
- Exactly 16 frames, one per cell, filled left to right, top to bottom, in the order of the numbered list below.
- Same character at the same scale in every cell. Standing Boppy is about 180 pixels tall. He gets shorter when hunched and wider when leaning, but never rescale him per frame.
- Every frame sits fully inside its cell with at least 10 percent empty margin on every side. Nothing touches or crosses a cell edge or overlaps a neighbouring cell.
- In every frame where his feet are on the ground, the soles rest on the same horizontal line, about 28 pixels above the bottom of the cell.
- Boppy faces LEFT (toward the viewer's left) in every frame, in a side or three-quarter view from slightly above, like a party member in a 16-bit battle scene facing an enemy off to the left.
- Background: one flat solid color, pure magenta #FF00FF, filling the whole image. No magenta anywhere in Boppy. No grid lines, borders, cell backgrounds, numbers, labels, captions or titles.
- Draw ONLY Boppy. No fire, flames, smoke, steam, sweat drops, sparkles, stars, speed lines, ground shadow, dust, motion blur or any effect outside his body (effects are added in the game, so show everything through his pose and face alone). No monster, no other people, no text, no word balloons.
- The only object allowed is one glossy red cayenne pepper with a curved body and a green stem, and only in frames 1 to 6.

ART STYLE
- 32-bit era high-detail pixel art (late SNES, Saturn, PlayStation quality): rich shading, 24 to 32 colors, clean readable silhouette, no anti-aliasing blur, no outline thicker than one pixel.
- A crisp one-pixel darker rim around his outer edge and a faint lighter highlight along his top edge.
- Soft key light from the upper left, shadow on the lower right, warm slightly desaturated painterly palette. All 16 frames share one look and one lighting direction.
- Family friendly for children aged 6 and up.

SKIN COLOR RULE
His normal face color is the same as in the reference in frames 1 to 6 and frame 16. Frame 7 is a pink flush. Frames 8 to 14 have a bright tomato red face from the chin up to the hairline, with the ears red too. Frame 15 is still blotchy red. Do not change his clothes color in any frame.

THE 16 FRAMES (each must look clearly different from the others)
1. WALK A: striding toward the left, left foot forward, right arm swinging, left hand held low in front of him gripping the red cayenne pepper by its stem, a slightly smug closed-mouth smile.
2. WALK B: the opposite step, right foot forward, body upright, the pepper held in his left hand at belt height, chin up, mustache bristling with pride.
3. PRESENT: standing still, feet apart, chest out, the pepper held up at shoulder height between thumb and two fingers like a trophy, a proud wide grin, eyebrows raised, eyes bright.
4. ADMIRE: pepper held close to his face in one hand, head tilted down to look at it, one eyebrow raised, the other hand on his hip, a smug satisfied smirk, as if saying "grew it myself".
5. RAISE: pepper lifted up in front of his open mouth, head tipped back a little, mouth stretched wide, eyes narrowed in determination, free hand clenched at his side.
6. CRUNCH: biting down hard, the pepper in his mouth with the green stem sticking out the left side, cheeks bulging, eyes squeezed shut, jaw tight, free hand clenched in a fist, body braced. The pepper is inside his mouth, only the stem and a bit of red show.
7. CHEW: both hands now empty and hanging at his sides, mouth closed and chewing, eyes slowly widening, face turning a pink flush, eyebrows lifting. The moment before the heat hits.
8. BURN: body stiff and rigid, both fists clenched at his sides, shoulders up around his ears, cheeks puffed and mouth clamped shut, eyes bulging wide, face bright tomato red.
9. FAN: one hand waving fast in front of his open mouth, the other hand pressed flat on his chest, tongue stuck out, eyes watering and squinted, face bright red, knees slightly bent, hopping in place with one foot off the ground.
10. INHALE: standing tall with chest puffed out enormously, head tipped slightly back, both arms pulled in tight with elbows bent and fists at his chest, cheeks full, nostrils flared, eyes narrowed and fierce, face bright red.
11. BREATHE A: leaning forward to the left, head thrust out, mouth open in a big round O, eyes squeezed shut, both arms stretched back behind him for balance, one foot sliding back, face bright red.
12. BREATHE B: the peak, body stretched far forward at a steep slant, neck stretched out, mouth wide open as big as it will go, eyes shut tight, arms straight back and fists clenched, back leg braced hard, cap tilting back, face bright red.
13. BREATHE C: easing off, still leaning forward but less steeply, mouth now a smaller round O, eyes streaming and half open, one hand rising to his throat, other arm hanging, face bright red.
14. GASP: hunched forward with both hands on his knees, head hanging low, tongue out and mouth hanging open, eyes watery and dazed, shoulders heaving, face bright red.
15. GRUMBLE: standing bent slightly forward, one hand clutching his belly, the other hand held out palm up in a complaining gesture, a grimace with the mouth turned down, eyebrows drawn together, face blotchy red.
16. SMUG: standing tall and straight, face back to its normal color, cap set straight, both hands on his hips, chin lifted, a satisfied half smile with one eyebrow raised, as if nothing happened.

NEVER substitute. Do not add extra frames, merge two frames, repeat a pose, or invent props. If anything is unclear, draw Boppy in the described pose.
