Remove those eight green boards completely (paint the wall, stone or brick that was behind each) and replace each with a sign that belongs to ITS OWN building: made of the same material, from the same era, in a lettering style chosen for that business. No two signs may share a style, color scheme or letter shape. They must feel painted, built or installed by different people over different decades.

RULES FOR ALL EIGHT
- Spell every word exactly as written. Use the case that suits the style, but every letter must be clear and readable on a small phone screen. Letters are large and bold enough to read at a glance.

- No other words, numbers, logos or tiny text. No new objects other than what each sign needs (bulbs, a bracket, chains, a small icon where listed).
- Same lighting as the rest of the painting: warm golden-hour light from the upper left, glowing where the sign is lit.
