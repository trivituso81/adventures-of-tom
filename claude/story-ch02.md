### The fight
- Monsters in this area and what each one was this morning: Smoglet (a puff of freeway smog), Ticket Flurry (parking tickets under windshield wipers), Canyon Coyote (a coyote), Gridlock (a car stuck in traffic), Tar Pit Blob (a puddle of tar). All far too bright.

- Why it has the coin: the coin was lying shiny in the grass by the tree. The hill sucked it in through a pipe and it has rattled there since.
- What it wants: company. A whole city lives on top of it, everybody is scared of it, and nobody has ever asked how it is. The one person who ever lay down on it and stayed a while is TNB.
