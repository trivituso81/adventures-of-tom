Draw the character in the attached pictures as one battle move sheet.

Picture 1 is the character. Picture 2 is their walk sheet. Pictures 3 and 4 are their battle sheets. Copy them as exactly as possible in every frame: the same person, face, age, body, head size compared to the body, hair, clothes, colors, painting style, level of detail, viewing angle and figure size as pictures 3 and 4. Only the pose and the objects with her change.

One image, a grid of 3 columns by 2 rows of equal square cells, on a flat solid magenta #FF00FF background. One figure per cell, the same size in every cell, feet on the same ground line, each figure well inside its cell. Held objects are painted in the same style as the character.

In every frame the character faces the same way as in pictures 3 and 4, in the same three-quarter view, unless a frame says she looks at the viewer.

The two plush lambs appear in EVERY frame. They are the same size (each about as big as her head) and must look clearly different from each other:
- LAMBY is old and well loved: wool gone dingy grey-beige and matted flat in places, a faded square patch stitched on its tummy, one ear worn thin and droopy, one button eye a different color from the other, a faded pink bow tie that is loose and crooked.
- BACKUP LAMBY is new: bright clean white fluffy wool, both ears perky, two matching shiny black button eyes, a crisp bright pink bow tie.
LAMBY is always on her LEFT side and BACKUP LAMBY is always on her RIGHT side. Never swap them, never leave one out, never make them look alike, and the two lambs never merge into one shape.

The big picture book: a large hardcover children's picture book with a plain soft blue cover and a ribbon bookmark. Inside, the pages show simple pastel pictures of a sleepy lamb under a moon. No readable words anywhere on the book.

The move: Bedtime Story.

Row 1:
1. Walking, the big picture book hugged against her chest in both arms, LAMBY (dingy, patched, droopy ear) tucked under her left arm and BACKUP LAMBY (bright white, perky ears) tucked under her right arm, both lamb faces peeking out, a sleepy smile.
2. Sitting cross-legged on the ground, the big picture book open on her lap, LAMBY sitting slumped on the ground at her left side and BACKUP LAMBY sitting upright on the ground at her right side, both lambs facing the book as if listening.
3. Sitting cross-legged, holding the open book up and turned outward to show the pictures, mouth open, reading aloud, LAMBY at her left side and BACKUP LAMBY at her right side, both looking up at the book.

Row 2:
4. Sitting cross-legged, turning a page with one hand, eyes wide, a "what happens next" face, LAMBY leaning against her left knee and BACKUP LAMBY leaning against her right knee.
5. Sitting cross-legged, the book open on her lap, a big yawn, one hand over her mouth, eyes closing, LAMBY tipped over sideways asleep at her left side and BACKUP LAMBY slumped sleepily at her right side.
6. Sitting cross-legged, the closed book held against her chest with LAMBY snuggled in her left arm and BACKUP LAMBY snuggled in her right arm, eyes shut, a soft smile, mouth softly saying "the end". No speech bubble and no written words.
