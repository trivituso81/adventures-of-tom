The environment should look as though it belongs in **Final Fantasy IX**: a whimsical, richly imagined fairy-tale world with theatrical architecture, charmingly exaggerated proportions, intricate decorative details, warm nostalgic lighting, and the distinctive hand-painted aesthetic of classic Final Fantasy IX backgrounds.

This is **one complete, walkable game map**: the whole ground floor of the club, painted as a single cutaway illustration with several connected rooms. The rooms are painted EMPTY: the game adds its own furniture, objects, characters and monsters afterward, so every floor stays completely clear.

## ART STYLE: FINAL FANTASY IX

Recreate the visual language of the hand-painted, pre-rendered environments and illustrated concept art of **Final Fantasy IX**, particularly its ornate interiors, theatrical towns, and atmospheric fantasy architecture.

- **Whimsical storybook fantasy:** A richly detailed, hand-painted environment with slightly exaggerated architecture, playful asymmetry, gently distorted proportions, and a magical, lived-in quality. Everything should feel lovingly designed, tactile, and full of personality.
- **Painterly rendering:** Visible, confident brushwork; chunky brushed shading in a few clear tonal families; delicate painted highlights; rich surface textures; and a traditional watercolor-and-gouache illustration sensibility. Avoid overly smooth digital gradients.
- **Expressive contours:** Dark brown and deep plum contour accents around important architectural details and decorative elements. Contours should feel hand-drawn and integrated into the painting, not like uniform comic-book outlines.
- **Rich, warm colors:** Deep jewel tones, honeyed golds, aged brass, burgundy velvet, midnight blue shadows, creamy marble, and warm amber highlights. Saturated but harmonious, with atmospheric color variation and an inviting fairy-tale warmth.
- **Fantasy architecture:** Slightly oversized doors, curved woodwork, ornate brass trim, sculptural pillars, decorative arches, and gently irregular craftsmanship. The club should feel like a charming, eccentric establishment from the world of Final Fantasy IX, rather than a historically accurate 1970s interior.
- **Warm golden illumination:** Soft golden light from the upper left, gentle amber glow around wall sconces, luminous edges on polished brass, and subtle cool violet-blue shadows. Rich painted ambient occlusion where walls meet the floor.
- **Intricate environmental storytelling:** Scratched parquet, tarnished brass, faded gilding, and dusty marble, with a fine layer of dust on the floor edges and wall ledges and a few faint cobwebs in the upper corners, suggest a once-splendid venue that has slowly fallen into disuse. Magical atmosphere without overt magical effects.
- **Visual clarity:** Every built-in feature should have a readable silhouette and sufficient contrast against the floor. Fine ornamental detail must never compromise navigation or spatial readability.

**Important:** The illustration should resemble a beautifully painted Final Fantasy IX game background, not a modern 3D reconstruction of one.

NOT pixel art. NOT modern retro pixel graphics. NOT photorealistic. NOT a 3D render. NOT anime cel shading. NOT flat cartoon art. NOT vector illustration. No pixelation, dithering, or artificially limited color palette.
