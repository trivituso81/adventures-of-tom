## Scene 1. TNB's loft
Idea: Tom insists TNB go first, then can't hold his news in. To TNB the purple sky is a detail and the bread is the problem.

- TNB: No, yours wins. How big a fish?

- TNB: Nara's already on it. She's been barking at it from your porch since breakfast.

## Scene 2. Tom's porch
Idea: Tom finds his poking stick. Nara thinks every stick is for her.
[Nara is lying against the front door. She sees Tom, jumps up, puts both front paws on his chest and licks his chin.]
- Tom: Nara, no jumping. Hello. Yes. Hello. That's my whole face. Thank you.
[Tom picks a long stick up off the lawn. Nara drops flat, tail going, eyes on the stick.]
- Tom: No. This isn't that kind of stick. This is a work stick.
- TNB: She thinks you're sticking around.
- Tom: Ha! I am. Right after we gently poke a cloud.
[Nara goes stiff. She stares down the street and growls. Purple smog is rolling down 2nd Avenue.]
- TNB: Huh. Now it's between us and the bread.
- Tom: Okay. Work stick. This is what you're for.

- Tom: Gold. And there's a lemon stamped on it.

[On the way down they pass the tree. It is still grey. The lemon is still in its block.]
