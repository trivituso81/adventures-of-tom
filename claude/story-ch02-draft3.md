## Scene 1. TNB's loft
Idea: Tom insists TNB go first, then can't hold his own news in.
[TNB is at the window with a grape soda. Tom comes in with his bag.]
- TNB: Hey, babe. You've got your news face on.
- Tom: I do have news. But you go first. I can wait.
- TNB: Sure. So I got up this morning, and I was thinking about toast...
- Tom: A FISH was wearing my mom's muffin pan. As a crown. I'm so sorry. Please. Toast.
- TNB: No, yours wins. How big a fish?
- Tom: Big. No, sorry. TAXI big.
- TNB: Huh. That'd tip the scales.
- Tom: Ha! It WOULD. Okay. Your turn. Toast.
- TNB: Never made the toast. The sky's purple.
- Tom: The sky is PURPLE. And I cut that off for a fish.
- TNB: It's a good fish. Smog's thicker up by the hills. I figure we go poke it.
- Tom: Poke it. Yes. Politely, and from the far end of a stick.
- TNB: Nara's coming. She's been on your porch since breakfast.

## Scene 2. Tom's porch
Idea: Tom finds his poking stick. Nara thinks every stick is for her.
[Nara is lying against the front door. She sees Tom, jumps up, puts both front paws on his chest and licks his chin.]
- Tom: Nara, no jumping. Hello. Yes. Hello. That's my whole face. Thank you.
[Tom picks a long stick up off the lawn. Nara drops flat, tail going, eyes on the stick.]
- Tom: No. This isn't that kind of stick. This is a work stick.
- TNB: She thinks you're sticking around.
- Tom: Ha! I am. Right after we poke a cloud.
[Nara goes stiff. She stares down the street and growls. Purple smog is rolling down 2nd Avenue.]
- TNB: Huh. It's never come down this far.
- Tom: Okay. Work stick. This is what you're for.

## Scene 3. 2nd Avenue
Idea: Mr. Washington is stuck on one greeting, and Tom keeps answering it properly.
[Mr. Washington is on his porch in the smog.]
- Mr. Washington: Morning, Tom! Back from your folks? How's your mama?
- Tom: Morning, Mr. Washington. She's well, thank you.
- Mr. Washington: Morning, Tom! Back from your folks? How's your mama?
- Tom: Still well! Thank you.
- Mr. Washington: Morning, Tom! Back from your folks? How's your mama?
- Tom: She's... consistently well.
- TNB: Babe. He's stuck.
[A small purple cloud with a face drifts between them. It coughs on Tom's shirt.]
- Smoglet: Ahem. You're in my way.
- Tom: Okay. So. Could you please cover your mouth? You do have one. I can see it.
- TNB: Huh. So that's a monster. Smaller than I pictured.
[A second cloud floats up beside the first.]
- Smoglet: Ahem. There's two of us.
- Tom: We can do this! I've done this exactly once.
[Fight: two Smoglets.]

## Scene 4. The boulevard
Idea: Doña Carmen thinks Tom can't cook. In the smog she says he can, and he keeps the compliment.
[Smoke rises from Doña Carmen's griddle. Miss Odette stirs a pot next door.]
- Miss Odette: This gumbo is perfect. Doesn't need a thing.
- TNB: She's never said that. Last week she sent a bowl back to herself.
- Doña Carmen: Everything's fine. Everything's fine.
[The pupusas are burning. Tom steps behind the counter and flips them.]
- Doña Carmen: Perfect. You cook so well, Tom.
- Tom: Did you hear that? She said I cook WELL.
- TNB: She's in the smog, babe.
- Tom: I know. I'm keeping it.
[More smog pours down from the hills. The pipes on the Observatory are puffing purple.]
- TNB: There. It's coming out of the Observatory.

## Scene 5. The trail
Idea: Tom gets a parking ticket from a monster and tries to contest it the proper way.
[Parking tickets swirl up off the trail and form a face.]
- Ticket Flurry: No parking Thursdays. Noon to two. You parked.
- Tom: I was in New York on Thursday. I still have my boarding pass.
- Ticket Flurry: Denied.
- Tom: Denied?
[Fight: Ticket Flurry.]
[Further up, a coyote steps onto the trail. It is far too bright. It looks only at Nara.]
- Canyon Coyote: Dog. Run with us. We never sit.
[Nara plants herself in front of Tom and barks once, very loudly. She is leaning back on his shins.]
- Tom: She's standing on my feet. Both of them. I'm going to hold very still.
- TNB: Easy. She's spoken for. You want the rest of my soda?
[The coyote growls.]
[Fight: Canyon Coyote.]

## Scene 6. The grey lemon tree
Idea: The cloud didn't bother TNB at all. The block does.
[Everything on the hill is purple except one small tree. It is grey. One lemon hangs inside a hard orange block.]
- Tom: It's the same as the dragonfly at the pond. Same block. Same little dent.
[Tom taps the block with the stick. Nothing happens.]
- Tom: Excuse me. Sorry. I had to check.
[TNB knocks on it with his knuckles.]
- TNB: It's not glass. It's not resin. I don't like it.
- Tom: You don't like it? You liked the talking cloud.
- TNB: The cloud made sense.
[Nara drops something on Tom's shoe and backs up. It is a burnt flashbulb.]
- Tom: A burnt flashbulb. There was one at the pond too. I'm texting Grace.
- Grace (text): Don't touch it, Tata. Take a picture. I'm putting it on my clue list. In pencil.
- Tom: Picture taken. Not touching. I'm two feet back. Two and a half.

## Scene 7. The Big Shake
Idea: Tom's body reacts before he does.
[The ground hums. The hill under the Observatory stands up. Two eyes open.]
- Tom: GAH! I've picked up Nara. I don't remember deciding to.
- The Big Shake: RUMBLE. A flash woke me up. Now everybody runs.
- TNB: Well, well, well... look who's up.
- The Big Shake: RUMBLE. Stay back. I shake.
- Tom: That's a whole hill. Good news, he's hard to miss.
[Boss fight: the Big Shake.]

## Scene 8. After the fight
Idea: The hill has known TNB for two years. TNB didn't know.
- Tom: Excuse me. Mr. Shake. How are you doing?
- The Big Shake: RUMBLE. ...Nobody asks. They just run.
- TNB: I don't run. I've been sleeping on you every Sunday. Sorry. I thought you were a hill.
- The Big Shake: RUMBLE. That was you? You snore.
- TNB: Yeah. That's fair.
- The Big Shake: My pipe itches. Hold on. Hh... HHH...
[The hill sneezes. A gold coin shoots out of a pipe. Nara catches it in the air, trots one full circle, and looks back at Tom.]
- Tom: Yes. I saw. Very good. May I?
[Nara drops the coin in his hand.]
- Tom: A gold coin. There's a lemon on it.
[Tom puts it in his pocket. The hill sits back down. The smog thins and the sky turns blue.]
- TNB: Sky's blue again. Shame. The purple was growing on me.
[On the way down they pass the tree. It is still grey. The lemon is still in its block.]
- Tom: Hm. That didn't fix the tree.

## Scene 9. 2nd Avenue again
Idea: Everyone is back to normal, so Doña Carmen takes the compliment back.
[Blue sky. Mr. Washington is on his porch.]
- Mr. Washington: Morning, Tom! Back from your folks? How's your mama?
- Tom: She's well, Mr. Washington.
- Mr. Washington: Glad to hear it. Tell her I said hello.
- Tom: A second sentence! Welcome back, Mr. Washington.
- Doña Carmen: Tom! Who turned my pupusas? They're all wrong.
- Tom: You told me I cook well.
- Doña Carmen: I never said that.
- TNB: You said it. I was there.
- Doña Carmen: Then I wasn't feeling well.
- Miss Odette: Sit down, both of you. This gumbo needs salt and you two need lunch.
[They sit. A pupusa is gone from Tom's plate. Nara sits very straight beside it with crumbs on her nose.]
- TNB: Good. I never did get my toast.
