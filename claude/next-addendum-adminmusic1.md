# adminmusic1 and adminmusic2 (2026-11-14, main 0d420ef): calm music on every admin screen

Tom: "I want to add music in the music library that is for whenever any admin screen is open and it's playing it within any admin screen. It should be soft, gentle, relaxing, sort of just giving the feeling of expansiveness and just calming."

## What exists
- Music Library track `admin` ("Admin Calm", group Admin, use Admin, loop, 87 s, 39th track): music/admin.mp3 is Tom's ElevenLabs take (export of 2026-11-14 15:02, merged in adminmusic2). The first, synthesized stand-in (adminmusic1) and its script were deleted. Prompt is in tools/music-library/tracks.json (auto: true).
- In the game: `mcAdminScreen()` in engine/music-clips.js (ADMIN list, Battle Lab list and its diff/solo pages, Welcome layout editor). A Battle Lab fight keeps battle music.
- Separate pages (voices, art, music, areas, portraits, heads .html): engine/admin-music.js, one script tag each (`?v=admin1`; bump it if the file changes). Follows `le-music`, carries on across pages (localStorage `aot-admin-music`), quiet while a clip or track plays or the mic is open, quiet when hidden, plays a phone take marked In the game.
- Not covered: recast-deedee.html (generated by tools/voice-recast/build-deedee.js).
- Test: `python3 tools/admin_music_test.py`. `tools/music-library/test.py` counts are 39.
- Note: the take starts quiet (a fade-in) and ends at full level, so the 2.5 s loop crossfade dips about 2 dB once per loop. Fine for a pad; re-make or trim only if Tom hears it.
