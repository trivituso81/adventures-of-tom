|---|---|---|

| The word "amber" | Used only as a color name. Grace matches it to the color the Long Island gate turned in chapter 11, Tom says the word. After that they say "the amber stuff" or "the blocks". | Nobody has to know what it is. They only know what color it is. |

## SCENE 1. The street (ch12Marlowe)

*2nd Avenue, outside Tom's house. A man in a grey suit stands in the middle of the road with a clipboard.*

**MARLOWE:** Marlowe. Monochrome Marlowe. I work for Mr. Vance. I fix places. This one has too much going on. Too much color, too many porches, and a dog doing something on a lawn.

**TNB:** That's Nara. She's allowed. It's a lawn.

**MARLOWE:** That's the problem with a neighborhood. Notes.

**GRACE:** What notes? What does that mean?

**MARLOWE:** A note is when I write down what's wrong with a place. Then I take it out.

*He holds up the clipboard. The top page says NOTES: LESS.*

**TOM:** Take it out how?

*He snaps his fingers. The pink goes out of the house across the street. Then the yellow. A song playing in somebody's window stops in the middle. The whole block is grey and silent.*

**ROSE:** Hey! Put it back!

**MARLOWE:** I don't put things back. Mr. Vance likes it quiet. Quiet is safe.

**AMY:** Safe from what?

**MARLOWE:** From everything, ideally. Mr. Prime will handle the intersection. Do take a look at the traffic. It's very stationary.

**ROSE:** What's stationary?

**TOM:** Stopped. He stopped the cars.

*Marlowe walks off into the grey, humming a jingle that never ends properly.*

**GRACE:** Why is it so quiet?

**TNB:** No music. No birds. I can't even hear the freeway.

**ROSE:** Tata, your neighbor isn't waving back.

*Mr. Washington is on his porch with one hand half raised. He is inside a block of something yellow and see-through, like a bug in a cough drop.*

**TOM:** Mr. Washington? It's Tom.

*The family walks up to the porch. Nothing.*

**ROSE:** Is he dead?

**AMY:** Rose! ...No. I don't think so. He looks like he's about to say hi.

**GRACE:** What is that stuff?

**TOM:** I don't know.

*Tom knocks on it. It sounds like knocking on a kitchen counter.*

**TOM:** It's hard. Like glass.

*TNB puts his hand flat on it.*

**TNB:** It's warm. Not hot. Like a mug that just had coffee in it.

**ROSE:** MR. WASHINGTON! CAN YOU HEAR ME?

*Nothing.*

**GRACE:** Did the grey man do this?

**TOM:** He snapped his fingers and then this happened. So, probably.

**GRACE:** Can we break it?

**TNB:** With him in there? Hang on. I'll try the corner.

*TNB taps the corner of the block with his skateboard. Then hits it. Not a mark.*

**TNB:** Nothing. My board has a dent now.

**ROSE:** Can't we melt it?

**TOM:** With what? It's sitting in the sun and it isn't melting.

**TNB:** I have a kiln.

**AMY:** You are not putting an old man in a kiln.

**TNB:** I wasn't going to. I'm just saying I have one.

**GRACE:** Tata. It's that color again. The Long Island gate turned that color right before it closed.

**TOM:** Amber. Yeah. It's the same.

**AMY:** Vance said he stops places. I thought he meant... I don't know what I thought he meant. I didn't think he meant this.

**ROSE:** Is Deedee in one of these?

**TOM:** I don't know, Rosie.

**TNB:** Babe. If it got him, who else did it get?

**TOM:** Doña Carmen's is right there. The Alvarez house is down the block. We check on everybody. Then we figure out how to get him out.

*Tom turns back to the porch.*

**TOM:** We're coming back, Mr. Washington. Don't go anywhere. ...Sorry.

## SCENE 2. Doña Carmen's (ch12Carmen)

*The pupuseria. Grey and silent. The comal is still on and the pupusas on it are starting to burn. Doña Carmen is in a block from the floor to over her head, one finger in the air, mouth open.*

**TOM:** Doña Carmen?

**GRACE:** Her too.

**ROSE:** Her mouth is open! She was talking!

*TNB knocks on the block, then lays his hand on it.*

**TNB:** Same stuff. Hard. Warm.

**GRACE:** Tata, look. It's dripping.

*The side of the block closest to the comal is shiny and running, like honey down a jar.*

*TNB pokes it. His finger goes in.*

**TNB:** It's soft on this side. Over here it's a rock.

**ROSE:** I SAID melt it.

**AMY:** You did say that.

**TOM:** So heat works. It has to be stove hot. Not nice day hot.

**TNB:** We can't push her onto the stove.

**AMY:** Then we bring the stove to her.

*Amy grabs a pupusa off the comal with the spatula and presses it against the block by Doña Carmen's hand. It sinks in and leaves a hole the size of a pupusa. Then it cools, and it stops.*

**AMY:** It worked until it cooled off.

**GRACE:** Do another one!

*A second pupusa. A bigger hole. Doña Carmen's hand is out in the open air. It doesn't move.*

**ROSE:** Her hand's out! Why isn't she moving?

**TOM:** I don't know.

*Tom steps in close.*

**TOM:** Doña Carmen. It's Tom, from the green house. Your pupusas are burning. You'd hate that.

*The edge of the hole sags.*

**GRACE:** It got softer when you talked! Keep talking!

**TOM:** About what?

**TNB:** Anything. You talk to the TV. Talk to her.

**TOM:** Doña Carmen, this is my sister and my nieces. They've never had a pupusa. I told them the curtido is optional.

*The block creaks.*

*Nara sniffs the smoke coming off the comal and sneezes. It is a very big sneeze.*

*A crack runs up the block like sugar glass. Then another. Then it falls away in warm gold flakes.*

**DOÑA CARMEN:** ...and THEN I told the man, the loroco is not optional. Tom! When did you get here? Why is everything so quiet?

**DOÑA CARMEN:** Where did my pupusas go?

**AMY:** We used them. I'm so sorry. Hi. I'm Amy.

**TOM:** You were stuck. Inside a big block of yellow stuff. A man in a grey suit did it.

**DOÑA CARMEN:** I was what? I had a whole sentence. I lost the sentence. It was a good sentence.

**DOÑA CARMEN:** That man stood on my step and said my sign was "too busy." I said the sign is busy because the food is good.

**GRACE:** So it's two things. Hot, and then loud.

**TNB:** Hot makes it soft. A big noise cracks it. I don't know why. That's just what happened.

**ROSE:** Nara did it.

**TOM:** Nara did it.

**TOM:** Doña Carmen, Mr. Washington's stuck the same way. He's out on his porch.

**DOÑA CARMEN:** Washington? He owes me for Tuesday.

*She takes a hot pan off the comal in one hand and a wooden spoon in the other.*

**DOÑA CARMEN:** Go next door. The Alvarez house. They didn't get any pupusas today. Take these. Feed the children first.

*She walks out. From the street: a pan banging, and a lot of Spanish. Then a slow, deep voice says, "Well. Afternoon, Carmen."*

**TNB:** She's faster than we were.

## SCENE 3. The Alvarez kitchen (ch12Alvarez)

*A small kitchen packed with people, every one of them in a yellow block. Mrs. Alvarez is at the stove with her finger up. Don Beto is in his chair pointing at the TV. Daisy runs in from the hallway.*

**DAISY:** Tom! Tom! Everybody stopped! Abuela stopped in the middle of saying NO!

**GRACE:** Why aren't you stuck?

**DAISY:** I don't know! I was in the bathroom!

**TOM:** It's okay, Daisy. We know how to fix it. We just need something hot.

*Amy takes a pupusa out of the bag and presses it on the nearest block. It leaves a thumbprint and nothing else.*

**AMY:** These are warm. They're not stove hot anymore.

**TNB:** So we use the stove.

*Mrs. Alvarez's block covers the whole stove. The knobs are inside it with her.*

**TNB:** Never mind.

**TOM:** Microwave?

**DAISY:** It doesn't turn on. Nothing turns on. I tried the TV.

**ROSE:** I'm hot.

**AMY:** Not now, Rosie.

**ROSE:** I'm HOT. I ran the whole way here.

*Amy looks at Rose's red face. Then at the size of the kitchen.*

**AMY:** ...When the girls can't sleep, I run them around the house until they're sweating. This kitchen is tiny. If we all run in here, it's going to get hot fast.

**GRACE:** And loud.

**TNB:** That's not stove hot.

**AMY:** Do you have a better idea?

**TNB:** I do not.

**TOM:** I'll hold the pupusas.

**AMY:** You'll run.

**AMY:** Girls. Laps. Around the table. As loud as you want.

**ROSE:** AS LOUD AS I WANT?

*They run. Amy, Grace and Rose, around and around the table. Daisy joins in. Nara barks at all of them. Rose screams the entire time. The windows fog up. The blocks go shiny. Then they start to drip.*

**GRACE:** Mommy, it's dripping!

**AMY:** Don't stop! Knees up!

*Crack. Crack. Then every block in the kitchen goes at once.*

**MRS. ALVAREZ:** ...NO. I said no. Why is everyone in my kitchen? Is that a mother running laps with two little girls?

**DON BETO:** ...and that is a FOUL! That is a foul! Who has the remote?

**TONY:** Why is there a woman running around my table? Why is it a good idea? It's a very good idea.

**AMY:** Hi. I'm Amy. Tom's sister. You were all stuck in yellow blocks. We had to make it hot in here.

**MRS. ALVAREZ:** ...Okay. That one can stay.

**DAISY:** Is it okay if I say that was the best thing that has ever happened in our kitchen?

**ROSE:** Again! Run with us, Mommy!

*Tombo unlocked: Run With Us, Mommy.*

**MRS. ALVAREZ:** Take lemons. Take ALL the lemons. Every yard in this city has too many and somebody always leaves them on my step.

**TNB:** Hot and loud. That's twice it worked.

**TOM:** Good. I think there's a lot more of that stuff out there.

## SCENE 4. After Gridlock Prime (ch12After)

*The intersection. Cars are moving and honking again. The pink is back in the window across the street. Marlowe is looking at his clipboard.*

**MARLOWE:** Love it. Hate it. Love it. Notes withdrawn.

**ROSE:** Does that mean we won?

**MARLOWE:** It means I'm leaving this street alone. It's too loud to fix.

**TOM:** You work for Vance. Vance closed Long Island. My mother isn't answering her phone. Where is she?

**MARLOWE:** Long Island is under glass, you know. A very safe house.

**GRACE:** What does "under glass" mean?

**MARLOWE:** Mr. Vance put a dome over the whole town. It's made of the same thing your neighbors were in. Nobody goes in and nobody comes out.

**AMY:** Is our mother in one of those blocks?

**MARLOWE:** No. She's in her kitchen, making a crisp. When it's done she starts it again. She's been making the same one for hours, and she doesn't know it.

**TOM:** How do we get in?

**MARLOWE:** You don't. That's what a dome is for.

**MARLOWE:** I'll be Downtown, at the Movie Palace. Come by. I'll have notes for the rest of the family. Notes are due at five.

*He walks off.*

**ROSE:** I don't like him.

**TNB:** A dome made of that stuff. Over a whole town.

**GRACE:** We can't run laps around Long Island.

**AMY:** I could.

**TOM:** She could.

**TNB:** If we can't go through it, we go under it. Or around it.

**ROSE:** Is Deedee okay?

**TOM:** He said she's baking. That sounds like Deedee.

**TOM:** We're going to go get her.
