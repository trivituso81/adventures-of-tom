## Parts (decided 2026-10-20)

The full game is played as two Parts from the title screen: PART 1 = chapters 1 to 10 (Act I), PART 2 = chapters 11 to 20 (Acts II and III). PART 2 is locked until the device has Part 1 clear data, then starts from it with everything carried over. Engine: engine/story.js section 6 and engine/splash.js (built, see project-instructions.md, section Parts). What chapter sessions must do: **C10** ends its last scene with `{partCard:1, done:true}` then `{partClear:true}` (writes the clear data and the autosave; replaces the plain CHAPTER 10 COMPLETE ending). **C11** sets `CHAPTERS[11].start = {map, x, y}` (where Part 2 begins) and adds `SCENES.part2Open` (the Part 2 opening scene; beginPart2 plays it after the PART 2 and CHAPTER 11 cards). Part 2 content must work from any Part 1 clear save (levels, stamps, gear, flags as the player left them).
