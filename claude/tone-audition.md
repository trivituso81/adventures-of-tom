# Tone audition (step 2 of claude/dialogue-overhaul-brief.md, 2026-11-12 23:00)

One beat, written three ways: the family finds Mr. Washington on his porch. Same staging, 12 pages each, words only. Nothing here is built. Tom picks one or mixes; his pick drives claude/voice-sheet.md (step 3).

Invented for the audition, not canon: Mr. Washington's habits (lawn comments, waving), the mailbox story, Rose licking the amber. The word "amber" is left out on purpose until Tom decides who names it.

## A. Dry and understated
Register: deadpan. The adults joke because they're scared, and the kids can tell.

1. Tom: Mr. Washington? ...He's never once let me walk past without a comment on my lawn. Something's wrong.
2. Rose: He's in a thing.
3. Grace: He's in a see-through yellow thing, Rose. Be specific.
4. TNB: It's warm. Babe, come feel this. It's warm like a mug right out of the kiln.
5. Tom: I'm good. I have a policy about touching the neighbors.
6. Amy: You're doing the calm voice. You did the calm voice when you backed Dad's car into the mailbox.
7. Tom: And that turned out fine.
8. Amy: It did not turn out fine.
9. Rose: Is he dead?
10. Tom: No. Look. He's fogging it up from the inside. Dead people don't fog.
11. Grace: That's not how doctors check, Tata.
12. TNB: His watch is still ticking. It stopped him and left the watch. ...Carmen's is right next door.

## B. Fast family banter
Register: quick and overlapping. Siblings sparring, Amy running the sidewalk like a classroom, everybody a little rude out of love.

1. Amy: Okay. Who is that, and why is he in a giant cough drop?
2. Tom: That's Mr. Washington. He runs this block. Unofficially. Don't tell him I said unofficially.
3. Grace: He can't hear you. He's in a cough drop.
4. Tom: You don't know him.
5. TNB: Babe. It's warm. Feel it.
6. Amy: Nobody feel it. Hands in pockets. Everyone. Tom, that includes you.
7. Tom: I own a house.
8. Amy: Pockets.
9. Rose: I licked it.
10. Amy: ...Of course you did. And what did we learn?
11. Rose: It's not a cough drop.
12. TNB: So it's warm, it's hard, and it's not lemon. That's three facts. I'm checking on Carmen.

## C. Quiet and eerie
Register: the scene is actually unsettling. Few jokes, and the ones there are come from the kids saying the true thing.

1. Grace: Tata. Your neighbor isn't moving.
2. Tom: Mr. Washington? It's Tom. From across the street.
3. Rose: He's inside the yellow. Like the bug in Grace's rock book.
4. Grace: That bug is forty million years old.
5. Amy: Grace. Not now.
6. Grace: I'm just saying how long it can last.
7. TNB: It's warm. Tom, it's warm all the way through. Like somebody just got up from a chair.
8. Tom: He's waved at me every morning for six years. I never once went up the steps.
9. Amy: So go up the steps.
10. Tom: ...He looks happy. That's the part I don't like.
11. Rose: He's not happy. He's stuck. Your face can do both.
12. TNB: There's the same glow in the window next door. ...Come on. Keep the girls close.
