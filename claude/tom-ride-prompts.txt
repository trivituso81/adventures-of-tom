SHEET 1 OF 7: BATTLE STANCE, RUN, HURT, LOW
============================================================

IMAGE REQUEST: ONE POSE SHEET. Return exactly one image, nothing else.

ATTACH: two reference pictures. Picture 1 is TOM, the grown man. Picture 2 is ROSE, the little girl. If a third picture is attached (a ride sheet already finished and approved), it shows the exact faces, head sizes and scale to keep: match it.

WHAT THIS IS
Pose frames for a family-friendly fantasy role-playing game (the off-screen opponent in the battle poses is a friendly cartoon monster): TOM carrying his niece ROSE on his shoulders. Both are painted characters in the same hand-painted caricature style as their reference pictures. Draw TOM exactly like picture 1 and ROSE exactly like picture 2 in every frame: same faces, hair, clothes, colors, proportions, art style and level of detail. Only the poses and objects below are new. Do not redesign anything about either of them.

ART STYLE
- A hand-painted digital caricature illustration of real people, shown full body. Semi-realistic painterly rendering with smooth soft shading, glossy highlights on skin and hair, and finely painted hair strands. Clothing prints and patterns are drawn in clear detail so every motif is readable.
- Caricature proportions: an oversized head, a small body, small hands and small feet, large expressive eyes and a warm, slightly exaggerated expression. The exact head sizes are fixed in HEAD AND BODY PROPORTIONS below and override any other size guess. Each face keeps a clear likeness of the real person in the reference picture.
- Warm golden light from the upper left, a soft warm glow on the skin, a gentle rim light, rich saturated warm colors.
- Clean soft edges. No thick or black outlines.
- NOT pixel art, NOT retro game art. No pixelation, no dithering, no limited color palette, no flat cartoon shading, no anime style.

REFERENCE FIDELITY
- The attached reference pictures show the real people as painted game characters. Picture 1 is Tom, picture 2 is Rose. Use every attached picture.
- The FACE must be a faithful likeness of the face in its reference picture: the exact face shape, jawline, cheeks, forehead, nose shape and size, lip shape, smile, eyebrows (shape, thickness, spacing), eye shape, eye color and eye spacing, skin tone, and any facial hair. Exaggerate only the expression, never the individual features. Do not beautify, slim, age up or age down either face, and do not turn it into a generic face.
- The HAIR must match the reference exactly: color, length, parting, volume, texture and how it falls.
- The BODY must match the reference: build, shoulder width, arm and leg proportions, posture. Keep the hands and feet small.
- The OUTFIT, shoes, jewelry and accessories must match the reference exactly: every color, print, pattern, trim, seam and button. Do not simplify or invent clothing details.
- If something is not visible in a reference (the back of the outfit), extend it plausibly and consistently. Never invent a new accessory, hairstyle or color.

HEAD AND BODY PROPORTIONS (fixed numbers; the most important size rule after the face lock)
- Tom: standing height from the soles of his shoes to the top of his hair is 340 px. His head, measured from the top of the hair to the bottom of the chin, is 146 px, which is 43 percent of his height. A huge caricature head on a smallish body, so the whole figure is only about 2.3 heads tall.
- Rose: standing height is 279 px, about 82 percent of Tom's. Her head, top of hair to bottom of chin, is 120 px, also 43 percent of her own height, so her head is about 82 percent the size of Tom's head: clearly smaller than his, never equal and never bigger. She has a small child's body, small hands and small feet.
- Seated on Tom's shoulders the pair together stands about 435 px tall from Tom's soles to the top of Rose's head (1.28 times Tom's own height). Rose's head sits above and slightly behind Tom's head and is smaller than his.
- These head sizes stay the SAME in every frame. Never shrink a head to fit an action pose, never enlarge it for a close-up, never let perspective, leaning, jumping or crouching change it. If a pose makes the figure shorter or wider, only the body changes.
- Do not make either body bigger or either head smaller to look more realistic. Tom's hands and feet stay small, Rose's too.

FACE LOCK (the most important rule of this whole sheet)
- Before drawing each frame, look at the reference pictures again and copy each face from them. Both faces must be instantly recognisable as the people in the pictures in every single frame, with the same eyes, nose, mouth, brows, hairline, beard and head shape. Expressions change as the frame says; the faces never do.
- Both faces are fully visible in every frame (except frames marked as seen from behind). Nothing covers Tom's eyes, nose or mouth: Rose's feet hang down at his chest, never in front of his face, and her hands never cover his eyes.
- Both heads turn the SAME way as each other in every frame, as the frame says. Rose never faces the opposite way to Tom.
- Rose is a small child: never make her older, taller or more grown-up looking.

THE RIDE (in every frame unless a frame says otherwise)
Rose sits securely on Tom's shoulders in a conventional parent-and-child shoulder ride, her feet resting at his chest, with Tom's hands holding her ankles. Keep the same positioning and the same character scale in every frame. Rose's hands rest on top of Tom's head, or do what the frame says.

- Background: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF filling the whole image. No magenta anywhere on the characters or objects. Pink clothes and shoes must stay clearly pink, never magenta. No magenta glow, spill or fringe. No grid lines, borders, cell backgrounds, numbers, labels, captions or titles.

2. RUN A: Tom striding fast to the left, near foot forward, opposite arm swinging free (one hand still steadying Rose's ankle), determined face; Rose bouncing on his shoulders, both hands gripping the top of his head, hair flying back, delighted open-mouth grin.

4. HURT: Tom wincing, leaning back a little to the right, chin up, eyes shut tight, one foot lifted, both arms out for balance; Rose holding on to the top of his head with both hands, eyes wide in surprise, bouncing up a little on his shoulders.
5. LOW HEALTH: Tom kneeling on one knee, one hand on the raised knee, head hanging, a black eye around his visible eye, a tired grimace; Rose still seated, hugging the top of his head gently with both arms, a concerned little pout, leaning toward him.
