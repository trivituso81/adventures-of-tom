## The plain story as last shown to Tom (12:37). Start here.

What Julian does, and why: Julian has a camera that freezes whatever he photographs inside a hard orange block. Things frozen that way do not move and do not get older. His daughter Clara is nine and will not speak to him because he missed her birthday. He decides to freeze her for a few days, so she stays nine while he figures out how to make it up to her. He believes it is safe, because every test he ran seemed to unfreeze by itself after a few days. He is wrong: it was Tom's family unfreezing his tests, and he never knew. He brings Clara to his photo studio in Los Angeles, takes her picture, and she freezes. Days pass and she does not unfreeze. He has no idea how to undo it. That is the turn: he cannot get her back, so he decides everyone else should wait for her. If her town, her school and her best friend are frozen too, then whenever she wakes up she will not have missed anything. Her best friend is Grace. From then on he freezes things on purpose: Long Island, then Tom's street, then he tries to freeze Grace.

- Chapter 10: at the airport the family finds a whole plane frozen in orange and unfreezes it. At the end they see Julian boarding a flight to Los Angeles with a little girl (Clara, on her way to his studio).
- Chapter 11: Tom flies out of Long Island with the girls and Amy sneaking aboard. From the window: a flash, and the whole town is under an orange dome. Julian has frozen Long Island with Deedee and Boppy inside. The plane lands at another airport where TNB and Nara meet them. Julian walks through on his way back to Los Angeles: "Nobody ever has to leave."
- Chapter 12: Julian photographs Tom's street. Mr. Washington, Doña Carmen and the Alvarez family are frozen. The family gets the three coins back and unfreezes them one at a time.
- Chapter 13: the cab drivers know a tunnel under the dome. On the way the family learns where the camera came from. They come up inside a grey, frozen Long Island, and the only person walking around is Boppy.
- Chapter 14: Boppy is unfrozen because he was standing next to Julian when the picture was taken (the camera only affects what is in front of the lens). He agreed to it: Julian promised nobody in the family would ever get older. He has the coin that would unfreeze the town and will not give it up. The family talks him around, he hands it over, the dome breaks, Deedee and the town are back.
- Chapter 15: everyone has dinner together for the first time. Julian tries to photograph them through the window and misses.
- Chapter 16: in the town square Julian aims at Grace. Deedee and Boppy jump in front of her and are frozen together. Julian notices the family scrambling for the coin, grabs it himself, and leaves for Los Angeles.
- Chapter 17: the family climbs his building in Los Angeles to get that coin back.
- Chapter 18: on the top floor is his studio, and Clara, frozen. Now they understand why. Tom tells him nothing ever unfroze by itself, it was us. Julian does not believe him. He offers Tom the camera ("you'd never miss anything again"), Tom says no. Julian aims at the family, Tom steps in front, Tom is frozen. Julian throws the coins down the mail chute.
- Chapter 19: without Tom, the family goes down to the lost-mail office to find the coins. They find Tom's, the grandparents', and a third inside a returned card: the coin from Clara's picture, which Julian mailed to her with an apology, not knowing what it was. They unfreeze Deedee and Boppy.
- Chapter 20: they go back up, unfreeze Tom, and Grace goes to unfreeze Clara. The thing inside the camera fights them. They beat it, then get through to Julian with kindness alone. Clara wakes up and speaks to her dad.
