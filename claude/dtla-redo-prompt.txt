DOWNTOWN LA REDO: PASS 1 (THE MAIN PAINTING)

Paint one new image of Downtown Los Angeles for a family RPG, in a 16-bit era storybook style grown up into rich painted detail. Hand-painted brush texture, warm golden-hour light from the upper left, long soft shadows falling to the lower right, glowing shop windows and street lamps just coming on. Same mood as a cozy city at sunset, saturated but not neon. No people and no animals.

CANVAS
- 1920 x 1664 px, landscape (15:13), PNG. Painted to all four edges: no frame, border, vignette, letterbox bars, caption or watermark.
- All coordinates are pixels from the top left corner. 1 tile = 64 px. Anything may be off by up to 32 px, never more. If your output size differs, scale every number in proportion.

CAMERA (the most important rule, the old painting got this wrong)
- One fixed three-quarter view from the south, camera pitched about 35 degrees down, in PARALLEL (orthographic) projection, like a classic 16-bit RPG city.
- Every vertical edge is perfectly vertical. Parallel lines stay parallel. Nothing converges toward a vanishing point. No wide-angle or fisheye distortion, no tilt-shift blur, no rotation, no diagonal streets.
- Streets that run east to west are perfectly horizontal on the canvas. Streets and paths that run north to south are perfectly vertical.
- Every building, prop and vehicle uses this same angle. Each building shows its flat FRONT facade facing the viewer and its roof top as a shallow band above it. Do NOT show side walls, so neighbors never read as angled.
- Nothing may be cut off by a canvas edge unless it is a street or path that deliberately continues off the picture (the three exits below). Every building, tree, water tank and sign is fully inside the canvas. The edges are closed by low walls, hedges and palms (see EDGES).

LAYOUT (a strict grid; buildings sit exactly on these footprints)
Every building footprint is 320 px wide. The facade fills the full 320 px (a cornice may overhang up to 16 px). The DOOR LINE is the y where the front door meets the sidewalk. Building art rises from the door line, never above the maximum height listed (so nothing runs into the top edge or into the boulevard).

The gaps between buildings (x 960 to 1024 and x 1344 to 1408) are narrow dead-end alleys closed by a fence, dumpsters or a fire escape. They are not walkable.

Between the south-row buildings there is nothing but sidewalk planters and lamps (x 448 to 576 and x 896 to 1024 are open sidewalk, x 1344 to 1408 is open).

Wide and clear, no cars parked in the lanes.

SELF-CHECK BEFORE YOU FINISH
1. Are all vertical edges vertical and all streets perfectly horizontal or vertical?
2. Is every building fully inside the canvas, with no building sliced by an edge?
3. Do the eight door centers match the x values above, and does each sit on its door line?
4. Is every sign spelled exactly as listed, and is it at least 30 px clear of its door?
5. Do exactly three paths leave the picture (northwest steps, south street, east service street)?
6. Is the ground visible under and behind every palm, awning, board, canopy and shelter?
