STORYBOOK CHARACTER FROM A PHOTO
Attach: one photo of one person. Nothing else.

Repaint the person in the attached photo as an illustration in a warm, traditional children's picture book.

THIS IS A REDESIGN, NOT A FILTER
Do not trace, filter or paint over the photo. Do not keep photographic skin or pores. Draw a new illustrated character who is clearly this person.

WHAT TO KEEP FROM THE PHOTO (the likeness)
- Face shape, eye shape, nose, mouth, eyebrows and the features that make them recognizable.
- Hair: color, length, parting and overall shape.
- Glasses, beard, mustache, freckles or smile lines, if the person has them.
- Apparent age. A child stays a child, an adult stays an adult, an older person stays older, with their lines drawn lightly in ink.
- Body build, posture and how tall or small they read.
- Skin tone.
- The outfit: same garments, same colors, same patterns, simplified into clean painted shapes with no logos or printed text.

THE ART STYLE
- Medium: traditional watercolor with a fine ink drawing underneath, on warm textured paper. Transparent layered washes, soft wet-edge pooling where washes meet, faint paper grain inside the painted areas.
- Linework: thin, slightly uneven hand-drawn ink lines in warm sepia brown, never black. Darker on the outer silhouette and shadow side, lighter and broken on the lit side.
- Color: a warm, sunlit palette with gentle saturation. Clear and luminous, never neon and never muddy. Shadows are warm washes of a deeper tone of the same color, not grey.
- Light: soft warm golden light from the upper left, soft shadows to the lower right, small unpainted paper highlights on the hair, cheeks, nose tip and shoe tops.
- Face: friendly and simple. Clear warm eyes with a small white highlight, a small soft nose, a gentle mouth, a soft rosy blush on the cheeks.
- Proportions: natural, gently simplified. Head slightly larger than real life, hands and feet slightly smaller.
- Hair: a few large flowing locks with a handful of fine ink strands.
- NOT pixel art, NOT a 3D render, NOT photorealistic, NOT anime, NOT flat vector cartoon, NOT airbrushed or glossy. No thick or black outlines.

POSE AND FRAMING
- One character only, full body from the top of the hair to the soles of the shoes, nothing cropped.
- Standing relaxed, turned slightly to the right, arms loose at the sides, both feet flat on the ground, a warm natural smile.
- Character centered, filling about 80 percent of the picture height.
- The figure is closed by its fine sepia ink outline and all paint stays inside it.
- Background: one flat, solid, untouched pure magenta #FF00FF. No paper texture, scenery, ground, shadow, frame, text or signature. No magenta on the character.
- Tall portrait picture, 2:3.
