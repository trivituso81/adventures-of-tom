STORYBOOK CHARACTER FROM A PHOTO
Attach: one photo of one person. Nothing else.

Repaint the person in the attached photo as a hand-painted storybook character for a fairy-tale role-playing game, in the art style of the game Child of Light: soft watercolor washes, visible paper grain, delicate thin hand-drawn linework, gentle color bleeding at the edges, a quiet, dreamy picture-book mood.

THIS IS A REDESIGN, NOT A FILTER
Do not trace, filter or paint over the photo. Do not keep photographic skin, pores, wrinkles or realistic facial detail. Draw a new illustrated character who is clearly this person.

WHAT TO KEEP FROM THE PHOTO (this is the likeness)
- Hair: color, length, parting and overall shape.
- Glasses, beard, mustache or freckles, if the person has them.
- Apparent age. A child stays a child, an adult stays an adult, an older person stays older.
- Body build and how tall or small they read.
- Skin tone.
- The outfit: same garments and same colors, simplified into clean painted shapes with no logos or printed text.

HOW TO SIMPLIFY
- Face: small, simple, expressive eyes, a tiny understated nose, a small soft mouth, soft rosy cheeks. Friendly, calm expression.
- Head slightly larger than real life, slim simplified body, small hands and feet.
- Hair painted as a few large flowing shapes, not single strands.
- Clothing folds reduced to a few soft brush strokes.

RENDERING
- Watercolor and gouache on textured paper. Soft edges, layered transparent washes, a few fine darker lines to define the eyes, hands and silhouette.
- Rich, deep, jewel-toned colors with gentle contrast.
- Warm golden light from the upper left, soft cool shadow on the lower right.
- No thick outlines, no flat cartoon colors, no glossy airbrushed plastic look, no 3D render look, no photorealism, not pixel art, not anime.

POSE AND FRAMING
- One character only, full body from the top of the hair to the soles of the shoes, nothing cropped.
- Standing relaxed, turned slightly to the right, arms loose at the sides, both feet flat on the ground.
- Character centered, filling about 80 percent of the picture height.
- Plain, flat, pale cream background with nothing else in it. No scenery, no ground texture, no frame, no text, no signature. Only a small soft contact shadow under the feet.
- Tall portrait picture, 2:3.
