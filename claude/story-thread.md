## 1. The rules of the world

| Thing | Rule |
|---|---|
| The camera | One old camera is the only magic thing in the game. It does not take a picture of a thing. It keeps the thing. |
| What happens when it fires | The color runs off whatever it is pointed at, like wet paint. The spot goes grey and quiet. |
| The orange block | Part of that color sets hard around the thing. Whatever is inside cannot move and does not get older. A person inside can still see and hear. The family never says "amber". |
| Only what is in front | The camera only freezes what is in front of the lens. Whoever stands behind or beside it is left out. That is why Julian is never frozen, and why Boppy is the one person walking around inside the frozen town. |
| The block stays put | A block is stuck where it formed. It cannot be picked up or carried. |
| The coin | The camera punches one small round piece out of the block. It drops to the ground as a gold coin stamped with the picture just taken (a dragonfly, a fish, a rosebush). The block is left with a round dent exactly the size of a coin. |
| Monsters | The rest of the color soaks into whatever is nearby and wakes it up: animals, plants and objects become monsters. A monster is far too bright (the brownest mud, the greenest catfish). The boss is the nearest and biggest thing that woke up, and it grabs the coin because it is shiny. |
| A person can be soaked too | It happens once in the game: Boppy, standing beside the camera for its biggest picture and holding the coin. The color grows over him as gold armor (chapters 13 and 14). |
| The flashbulb | A burnt-out flashbulb is left on the ground next to every grey patch. |
| Breaking a block | Put its own coin back in the dent. It clicks in like the last piece of a puzzle, gold cracks spread from that spot, and the block falls apart. The coin is used up. Rose: "It's a gumball machine!" |
| Only its own coin | The coin carries that block's picture and is the piece that came out of that block. Any other coin will not click in. |
| What breaking does | The color runs back into the grey patch, every monster in that area shrinks back to the normal animal, plant or object it was, and the boss shrinks back too. That area has no monsters from then on, unless a new picture is taken there. |
| Growth | Each picture the camera takes is bigger than the last, whether Julian wants it or not. (Holds strictly through chapter 11. After the town, pictures are no longer in size order.) |

## 2. The camera's backstory (never stated outright, only word of mouth)

- Years ago a small meteor came through the roof of Penny's shop on Long Island and hit an old camera on the shelf. Something from it got into the camera. The game never explains further.
- Boppy tells it as a tall tale ("big as a Buick"). Deedee corrects him ("It was a pebble. It went through the pawn shop roof.").
- Visible clue: a patched hole in Penny's ceiling.
- Chapter 13: the family learns Penny sold Julian the camera that sat under that hole.
- Chapter 20: the thing from the meteor comes out of the camera and is the last big fight. Tom (13:42) likes this as the late twist: the final enemy is not the man who caused the trouble. His comparison is Golbez in Final Fantasy IV, who turns out to have been used by a greater force. Julian is the Golbez. The camera taking bigger pictures than he wants is the visible sign of it all game long. Seed it lightly, never explain it.
- Second seed (13:50): Boppy's gold armor in chapters 13 and 14 is the same gold as the thing that comes out of the camera in chapter 20. Nobody says so. The camera had already taken someone over once.

## 3. Julian Vance

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| Before the game | Julian is a photographer. He and his daughter Clara live on Long Island, in a different neighborhood from Rivituso Row that the player can never visit. He works in Los Angeles, flying back and forth, always away at other families' birthdays and weddings. He misses Clara's ninth birthday. She tells him "I'm not talking to you." The next day he photographs her leftover birthday balloon with an old secondhand camera from Penny's shop. The color runs off and sets hard around it. Weeks later the balloon has not gone down: things in a block do not get older. |
| His goal (Tom 12:55) | He wants to use the camera to stop things from getting older. The point was never to freeze Clara for a few days. He wants her to stay nine. |
| Chapters 1 to 9 | He tests the camera on bigger and bigger things, because he needs to know it is safe before he goes near his daughter with it. To him the coins are junk the camera spits out. |
| What he believes | The family unfreezes his tests. He comes back later (the player does not see this at the time), finds each thing out of its block and perfectly fine, and concludes two things: the block melts off by itself after a few days, and whatever was inside never ages again. He never knows it was the family. |
| End of Part 1 | He takes Clara to his Los Angeles studio for a make-up birthday portrait and takes her picture. He expects her to come out in a few days and stay nine forever. |
| Part 2 | She never comes out. That terrifies him. Everything he does in Part 2 is a frightened man trying to find out why, and making it worse. |
| The flashback | His return visits to the old tests are shown once, late, as a flashback in chapter 18 when Tom tells him the truth. |

- He is a careful father who got it wrong, not a villain experimenting. He works alone, with one camera.
- There is no seeing the future. He is simply afraid she will grow up still angry.
- Clara is alive and nine during Part 1. She goes to Grace's school, the chapter 5 school where Amy teaches. Grace knows her from school. They are classmates, NOT best friends. The built chapter 5 already has the man in the long coat in the school parking lot: he is there picking her up, late again.
- The family is part of the cause: their good deeds are what convinced him it was safe. They are also the only ones who know how to break a block.
- He never freezes anything on purpose "so Clara won't miss anything". That idea was dropped (Tom 12:46).

## 4. Tom and Julian (the foil)

- Both men split their lives between Long Island and Los Angeles. Julian missed things and tried to stop his daughter growing up. Tom is afraid of the same thing (the girls growing up too fast while he is away) and also wants the life he is building in Los Angeles with TNB. He keeps showing up anyway.
- Boppy is the third man with the same fear, and the one it actually takes over (chapters 13 and 14).
- Woven in subtly, said out loud only once:

| Flights | Tom looks at one photo on his phone. Flying east it is TNB, flying west it is the girls. |

| Chapter 11 | Julian to Tom: "You, of all people, know why." |
| Chapter 18 | Julian offers Tom the camera: "You'd never miss anything again." Tom turns it down. |

- The ending this sets up: Julian cannot free Clara. The family can. Grace is the age Clara is.

## 7. Part 2: the approved story, chapters 10 to 20 (Tom 13:42, chapters 13 and 14 expanded 13:50)

What is built today in each of these chapters is in the "Built today" column of claude/part2-remap.md. Use that column only. Its "New version" column and its list of new ideas are superseded by this section.

- Chapter 10 (end of Part 1). At the airport the family finds a whole plane frozen in an orange block and unfreezes it. The last thing the player sees is Julian boarding a flight to Los Angeles, holding a little girl's hand. He is taking Clara to his photo studio for a make-up birthday portrait.
- Chapter 11. Tom flies out of Long Island, and the girls and Amy sneak aboard. Meanwhile, off screen, Clara has been frozen for days and has not come out. Julian has flown home and photographed one small thing in his own front yard, to time how long melting really takes. The camera takes a bigger picture every time, and this time it takes the whole town. From the plane window the family sees a flash and Long Island under an orange dome, with Deedee and Boppy inside. They land at another airport, where TNB and Nara meet them. Julian walks through on his way back to Los Angeles. He tells Tom the town will melt off in a few days and nobody in it will ever get a day older: "You, of all people, know why." The family learns his name.
- Chapter 12. In Los Angeles, Julian goes back to Mr. Washington's rosebush on Tom's street. It was one of his earlier tests, and he wants to repeat it and time it. The camera takes Mr. Washington, Doña Carmen and the Alvarez family. This is the first time the family watches it happen, and they see that it works on people. They win back the three coins and unfreeze the neighbors one at a time.
- Chapter 13. The cab drivers know a tunnel that goes under the dome. On the way, the family learns the camera came from Penny's shop, from the shelf under the hole in her roof. The cab drivers also talk about a gold man who walks the frozen town. The family comes up inside a grey, frozen Long Island. Nothing is moving except one figure in shining gold armor, far off, who never speaks. The town's coin is set in his chest plate. The family assumes he is the boss, the biggest thing that woke up. They find the dent in the dome wall, the spot where the coin has to go. They fight the knight at the dent and win. The coin drops. Grace grabs it and runs to the dent. She is one second from freeing the whole town. A gold hand closes over hers. The helmet opens, and it is Boppy. "Don't, Gracie. I made a deal." He takes the coin back and the helmet shuts. Then the family sees what the town picture did: it spilled color over all of Long Island, so everything they fixed there in Part 1 has woken up again. The pond, the pier, the school, the park and the library are full of monsters, bigger and brighter than before. Every boss they befriended is back, and they stand with the knight. The chapter ends there.
- Chapter 14. How Boppy got this way: he was standing next to Julian when the town was photographed, and the camera only freezes what is in front of it. Julian told him what he believes: it melts off, and then nobody in the family ever gets older. Boppy picked up the town's coin. The town was the biggest picture the camera ever took, so it spilled the most color, and it soaked into him. It grew over him as gold armor, far too bright, like every monster. His wish that nobody in the family gets older is his own. The armor makes that one wish louder than everything else in him. The family gets past their old friends and reaches him. The last fight is one where nobody hits him, because it is Boppy. They break the armor by reaching him. It cracks, the color runs out of it, and he is himself again: "I just didn't want to get too old to keep you all safe." He hands Grace the coin himself. The dome breaks, Deedee and the town come back, and every monster on Long Island turns back for good.
- Chapter 15. Everyone has dinner together for the first time. Julian has now seen the town come back and the neighbors come back. Only Clara has not. He wants to know what happens to a nine-year-old girl, and Grace is nine and in Clara's class. He aims at Grace through the window, misses, and freezes the radio on the windowsill.
- Chapter 16. In the town square he aims at Grace again. Deedee and Boppy jump in front of her and are frozen together in one block. Julian sees the family scramble for the coin. For the first time he wonders if the coins matter. He grabs it ("Why do you want this?") and leaves for Los Angeles.
- Chapter 17. The family climbs his building in Los Angeles to get that coin back.
- Chapter 18. On the top floor is his studio, with Clara frozen in it. Julian is pushing the grandparents' coin into the dent in her block, and it will not click in. Now the family understands why he did all of it. Tom tells him the truth: nothing ever melted, it was us, and every block needs its own coin. The flashback plays here (Julian visiting his old tests and finding them open). Clara's coin is gone, because he mailed it home to her inside an apology card, thinking it was junk. He offers Tom the camera ("You'd never miss anything again") and Tom says no. Then Julian turns on them: he only thought it was safe because of what they did. He raises the camera at the family, Tom steps in front ("No touching, please.") and Tom is frozen. The coins go down the mail chute.
- Chapter 19. Without Tom, the family goes down to the lost-mail office to find the coins. They find Tom's, the grandparents', and a third inside a returned card addressed to Clara. It came back because Long Island was closed under the dome when it was sent. They unfreeze Deedee and Boppy.
- Chapter 20. They go back up and unfreeze Tom. Grace goes to put Clara's coin in her block. The thing that came from the meteor comes out of the camera and fights them, and all eight beat it. Then they get through to Julian with kindness alone. Clara wakes up and speaks to her dad.

What Tom asked for in Boppy's betrayal (13:48, his words tidied), so later sessions keep it: it must be a big, shocking moment that hits just as the family is about to fix everything, and it must undo work they have done. Boppy has a real reason but is also somewhat possessed, which is why he has the armor and acts unlike himself. There is build-up to a mysterious man in gold armor, a first battle, the helmet opening to show it is him, and a final battle to break the armor, which breaks the hold on him.
