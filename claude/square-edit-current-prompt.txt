Keep the painting style, lighting, palette, camera angle, perspective, paving, trees, lamps, benches, building positions, building footprints, roof colors, doors and all exits exactly as they are. Everything not listed below stays exactly the same. Output 1920 x 1664 px or the largest size the tool allows with the same aspect ratio, painted to all four edges, no border or watermark. Coordinates are pixels from the top left of a 1920 x 1664 canvas; anything may be off by up to 32 px.

A. GIVE EACH OF THE EIGHT BUILDINGS ITS OWN FUN DESIGN AND ITS OWN SIGN
Every building keeps its exact position, footprint, roof color and door, but gets a distinctive theme feature and a sign painted into its own materials, so no two buildings or signs look alike. Each sign is centered over its door, about 280 x 130 px, its bottom edge at least 30 px above the highest part of the door or awning, never covering a door, awning or roof emblem, drawn straight on and level. Letters are at least 38 px tall with thick strokes and strong contrast, ALL CAPITALS, spelled exactly letter by letter, two lines. Remove all old small text and blank boards. All eight use clearly different lettering styles, board shapes and materials.
1. Crumb Bakery (north row, far left): add a tall brick bread-oven chimney puffing soft smoke and a wooden bread cart with loaves by the door. SIGN: a giant wooden baker's bread peel (long paddle) above the door, the words piped on in thick white icing in bouncy, plump, swirly cursive. CRUMB / BAKERY.
2. Hush Library (north row, second): add a carved stone owl on the roof peak beside the existing gold book. SIGN: letters carved into a pale stone open book set above the door, filled with gold leaf, thin widely spaced classical Roman capitals. HUSH / LIBRARY.
3. Sprout House (north row, third): add more glass panes and climbing vines, a giant copper watering can and stacked terracotta pots by the door. SIGN: letters made of living twisting green vines with tiny leaves and pink blossoms along a curved white iron trellis arch, loose organic letterforms. SPROUT / HOUSE.
4. Penny's Pockets (north row, far right): keep the hanging gold coin (no writing on it). SIGN: a theater marquee board ringed with glowing round bulbs, bold circus-poster wood-type in gold, red and cream with a black drop shadow. The apostrophe sits between Y and S. PENNY'S / POCKETS.

SIGN: a hanging sign shaped like a big china teacup and saucer with delicate curly Victorian tea-tin lettering in cobalt blue on white porcelain with a gold rim. KETTLE / COTTAGE.
7. Moon Club (south row, third): keep the gold crescent moon and brass globe, add a small brass observatory dome and a telescope. SIGN: a polished brass crescent-shaped plaque above the door with glowing gold lettering like constellations, thin geometric Art Deco capitals made of lines and star dots, the two O letters drawn as perfect circles. B. A MUCH LARGER FOUNTAIN
The fountain in the middle of the plaza becomes the biggest, brightest object in the square: a round basin about 460 px across, thick scalloped pale cream limestone rim, mossy base, clear turquoise water with a few gold coins, a carved column with a second bowl and a third small bowl on top with a plume of water arcing out. It stays centered where the current fountain is, may grow in every direction, and nothing touches it: keep 40 px of open cobble all around, and keep the benches, lamps and trees where they are unless the fountain needs them moved by up to 60 px.

C. EXIT BOARDS
Keep the three green exit boards (GRUMP / HOLLOW north, RIVITUSO / ROW south, SCHOOL east) in the same style, but mount each HIGH on two tall posts (posts about 90 px taller than now) so the open ground is clearly visible beneath each board, and make sure each exit is a wide, clear opening (at least 130 px) with a worn trail visibly continuing off the picture. D. KEEP THE GROUND VISIBLE BEHIND AND UNDER THINGS (for layering in the game)
A figure will later walk behind trees, lamps, the fountain, awnings, roof edges and boards. So: paint ground fully under and behind every upright object; keep a strip about 40 px tall of visible ground behind each tall object's base; trees have clear trunks with visible bases and tidy closed canopy silhouettes with a slightly darker rim; keep at least 24 px between any two upright objects; do not paint tree canopies over doors, signs, the fountain or exits; keep ground shadows soft, short and low contrast so the ground stays clean when an object is cut away. Keep 64 px of clear cobble in front of every door.

Do not add people, animals, extra buildings, extra doors, extra freestanding signs or notice boards, closed gates, or any text other than the eleven signs listed above.
