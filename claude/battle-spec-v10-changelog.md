# The Adventures of Tom: Battle Spec v8 -> v10 Change Log

Every change from Gemini's v8 is listed. Names, jokes and subtitles were not touched. Five visual arcs were updated in v10.

## Changed in v10 (after the Gemini review of v9)

| Where | v9 | v10 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| VP curve | A mastered Vibe earned nothing. A perfect player ended Chapter 20 at 2,390 of 2,400 VP. | Overflow: a mastered Vibe's share goes to the lowest unmastered Vibe. 800 per Vibe kept. All three master around Chapter 18. | Gemini's simulation was right and my pacing claim was wrong. I kept 800 instead of Gemini's 650 because 650 with overflow masters everything by about Chapter 15. |
| Casual Mode | Unlocked after the first mastered Vibe and earned no VP, so equipping it stalled progress. | Unlocks after all three Vibes are mastered. | Gemini is right. It is the endgame reward. |
| Grace, NeeDoh Stretch Pass | Counted as the ally's one granted action per round. | Treated as a pull. Each ally can be moved this way once per round. | Gemini is right. It moves a turn, it does not add one. |
| Seven Capstones | -1 Composure, or -2 on a Pet Peeve hit or Detonation. | -2 Composure restored on Konami Code, Grand Jeté, To the Moon & Back, Leviathan, Grand Amphora, LivNews and Regional Championship Freestyle. | Gemini is right. A 6 Pep Capstone should out-shave a 2 Pep skill. The 3-pip cap still applies. |
| Stat Leans | None. | Guard, Striker, Tempo or Support on every Vibe, +15% as Main Vibe. | Gemini's idea, accepted. It was never in v7 or v8, so v9 did not drop it. |
| Two-flavor moves | Order undefined. | First flavor resolves first. Prime applies only if nothing detonated. | Gemini is right. |
| Story encounters | Not mentioned. | Rule 2.11 keeps chapter-defined Kindness commands. | Gemini is right that the spec needs the hook. They were not in the v8 file, so nothing was deleted. |
| Late joiners and roster | Synced to Tom. Assumed all ten present. | Synced to the highest-level party member. Rules work with any roster. | Covers chapters where Tom is out of the party and optional recruits. I could not check those story facts myself. |
| Five visual arcs | Described moves that had been cut. | Rewritten to match the current moves (Star Baker, Prankster, Classroom Boss, The Dep, Picky Princess). | Gemini is right. |
| Two author notes | '(no Lamby)' and '(recliner removed)' sat inside move text. | Removed. | Gemini is right. |
| En dashes | 15 in number ranges. | Left as they are. | Tom's call. |

## Rules (Sections 1, 2 and 4)

| Where | v8 | v9 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1, VP earning | Only the Main Vibe earns VP. 300 VP masters a Vibe. | Main earns full, Pocket earns half, bench earns too. A mastered Vibe passes its share to the lowest unmastered one. 800 VP masters a Vibe. Ranks at 0 / 60 / 220 / 450 / 800. | v8 numbers mastered everything by about Chapter 11. New numbers land first mastery near Chapter 9 and all three near Chapter 18. |
| 1, VP budget | 8 to 12 VP per fight, 30 per boss, no fight count. | About 90 Main VP per chapter, scaled if fight counts change. | Pacing no longer depends on an unstated number of fights. |
| 1, late joiners | Arrive with 'unspent VP' although VP was not spendable. | Arrive at the level of the highest-level party member with a pool equal to the highest total any character has earned, split once by the player. | Removed the contradiction. |
| 1, loadout | Pocket adds 'Active Skills'. Quirk source not stated. | Pocket adds Skills only (no Basic, no Trait). Quirks come from the character's own three Vibes. Sig means default Main and Pocket. | Filled gaps. |
| 1, Portable Counters | 'Lane-Agnostic Core Mechanics', builds unclear. | Renamed. Counter builds by the Trait's rule when pocketed. Other Trait bonuses are Main only. Cadence added to the list. | Finishes the v8 fix. |
| 1, Casual Mode | Named, never defined. | The reward for mastering all three Vibes: two Pocket slots, chosen Basic, all three Traits. | New definition, timed as the endgame reward. |
| 2.1, party and field | Not defined. Kits mention rows, lanes, adjacency. | Party of 4, Front and Back rows, slot adjacency, lanes removed, 'Mix' replaces 'cross-lane combo'. | Missing core rules. |
| 2.2, timeline | Round = 100 ticks. Turn durations and push percentages undefined. | Recovery formula, Base Recovery 80 / 100 / 120, turns versus rounds, push and pull as ticks. | Missing core rules. |
| 2.2, diminishing returns | Pushes on foes only. | Also halves repeat pulls on the same ally, limits set-spot pulls to once per ally per round, and limits a unit to one granted action per round. | Ally pulls and free turns were the remaining way to lock enemies out. |
| 2.3, Pep | Called 'strict', nothing limited. | Per-character Pep stated. Cap of +3 Pep per round from anything but your own Basic. Skill cost floor of 1. | Pep flood. |
| 2.4, damage and caps | No formula. | Damage formula, power tiers, crit, heal sizes, caps on damage bonus, reduction, evasion, shields and Defense cracks. | Missing core rules. |
| 2.5, Primes | Coexistence undefined. Dazzled 'lowers Crit threshold'. | Primes can coexist, a detonating hit does not re-prime, one Detonation per target per action, two-flavor moves resolve first flavor first. Dazzled is +15% Crit Rate taken. | Missing core rules. |
| 2.6, Detonations | Header said Detonations shave 2+, list gave 1 or 0. | Every Detonation shaves 1. Steam Cloud and Short Circuit shave 2. | Removed the contradiction. |
| 2.7, Composure | Flat shaves 'reduced to 1' while 11 kit lines said 2. | Skills 1 and 2 and Quirks print 1. Capstones keep 2. 3-pip cap per action. Flustered timing defined. | Removed the contradiction. |
| 2.8, aggro and statuses | Aggro, slow, stun, fatigue, bleed and mental used but undefined. No screen limit. | Aggro weights, a status glossary, and a 3-icon limit per unit. | Missing core rules and phone screen load. |
| 2.9, revive | Only Deedee and TNB could revive. | Pick-Me-Up, a 4 Pep revive every character has. | A party without those two had no recovery. |
| 2.10, Family Ruckus | Pair combos named, none written (45 pairs). | Fill values, plus two rule-driven combos (Double Team at 50%, Full Family Ruckus at 100%) that work for any pair. | Removes 45 custom combos from the build. |
| 1, Stat Leans | None. | Each Vibe has a Lean (Guard, Striker, Tempo or Support) worth +15% as Main Vibe. | New. Gemini asked for it. The 30 assignments are my reading of each Vibe's role. |
| 2.11, story encounters | None. | Story bosses can add their own Kindness commands, defined per chapter. | Keeps the built game's non-violent boss actions. |
| 4, build order | None. | Three passes of 40 moves. | Scope. |
| Whole file | 87 leftover [cite] tags, em dashes, 'dimishing' typo. | All removed. | Cleanup. |
