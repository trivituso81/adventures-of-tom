## Prompt text

IMAGE REQUEST: ONE SINGLE ANIMATION SHEET. Return exactly one image, nothing else.

WHAT THIS IS
Animation frames for "The Adventures of Tom", a family role-playing game with a classic 16-bit JRPG feel (Final Fantasy VI, Chrono Trigger) and hand-painted character sprites. Every frame shows Tom, the adult hero. Two images are attached: (A) the reference for Tom, and (B) the reference for his big over-ear noise-canceling headphones. Draw Tom EXACTLY like reference A: same face, hair, clothes, colors, proportions, art style, level of detail and line quality. Draw the headphones EXACTLY like reference B: same shape, colors and size relative to his head, identical in every frame where they appear.

THE MOVE
"Noise-Canceling": a monster is yelling at the party. Tom, calm and dry, takes out his headphones, puts them on, taps the noise canceling button, and enjoys total silence. He is unbothered, a little smug, a little blissful. In frames 1 and 2 the headphones are in his hands. In frames 3 to 16 he is WEARING them: the band over the top of his head and one ear cup over each ear, sitting snugly and correctly.

THE SHEET (strict)
- A grid of 4 columns by 4 rows of square cells, 256 x 256 pixels each, so the full image is 1024 x 1024. If your tool offers a different size, keep exactly 4 columns and 4 rows.
- Exactly 16 frames, one per cell, filled left to right, top to bottom, in the order of the numbered list below.
- Same character at the same scale in every cell. Standing Tom is about 200 pixels tall. Never rescale him per frame. Each frame sits fully inside its cell with at least 10 percent empty margin. Whenever his feet are on the ground, the soles rest on the same horizontal line, about 24 pixels above the bottom of the cell.
- Frames 1 to 13 and 16: Tom faces LEFT, in a side or three-quarter view from slightly above, like a party member in a 16-bit battle scene facing an enemy off to the left. Frames 14 and 15: Tom faces RIGHT (he is walking back to his spot).
- Nothing touches or crosses a cell edge or overlaps a neighbouring cell.
- Background: one flat solid color, pure magenta #FF00FF, filling the whole image. No magenta anywhere in Tom or the headphones. No grid lines, borders, cell backgrounds, numbers, labels, captions or titles.
- No ground shadow, sound waves, rings, music notes, text, speech bubbles, sparkles, stars, motion lines, motion blur or any effect floating outside the character. Show everything through pose and expression alone. No monster, no other people, no other props. The headphones are the only prop.
- If reference B has a small indicator light on an ear cup, it is off in frames 1 to 4 and glowing green in frames 5 to 16.

ART STYLE
Match reference A exactly. Do not restyle Tom. If reference A is pixel art, draw pixel art at the same resolution, palette size and outline weight. If it is painted, paint with the same brushwork, shading and outline. Same light direction as reference A in every frame, one consistent look across all 16 frames. Family friendly for children aged 6 and up.

THE 16 FRAMES (each must look clearly different from the others)
1. TAKE OUT: standing, the headphones held in both hands in front of his chest, band toward the top, looking down at them with a small, knowing half smile. Headphones NOT worn.
2. RAISE: both arms lifted overhead holding the headphones open above his head, band spread wide, chin tipped up, eyes on the headphones, calm and ceremonial. Headphones NOT worn.
3. LOWER: the headphones just landing on his head, band on top, both hands still gripping the ear cups, cups sliding down over his ears, eyes closed in quiet satisfaction.
4. ON: headphones fully on, hands dropped to his sides, standing tall, eyes open and level, calm and ready.
5. TAP: headphones on, one finger pressing the ear cup nearest the viewer, head tilted slightly toward that hand, eyes open, a tiny smirk.
6. MUTE: headphones on, one hand held up with the palm out toward the left like a polite stop sign, the other hand on his hip, eyes half lidded, one eyebrow raised, completely deadpan.
7. CAN'T HEAR YOU: headphones on, one hand cupped over the ear cup nearest the viewer, leaning slightly toward the left with a fake effort to listen, a flat unbothered mouth.
8. SHRUG: headphones on, both palms turned up in a slow shrug, shoulders raised, eyebrows up, mouth a flat line.
9. BLISS A: headphones on, eyes closed, serene smile, head tilted slightly back, arms loose at his sides.
10. BLISS B: headphones on, eyes closed, head tilted slightly to the other side, shoulders dropped and relaxed, a content little smile.
11. LOUNGE: headphones on, both hands clasped behind his head, leaning back slightly with his weight on his heels like reclining in an airplane seat, eyes closed, relaxed smile.
12. IDLE: headphones on, standing in his normal battle idle stance from reference A, eyes open, looking left, calm neutral mouth.
13. BLINK: exactly the same pose as frame 12, but with his eyes closed.
14. WALK A: facing RIGHT, headphones on, mid stride with his left leg forward, arms swinging gently, relaxed and a little smug.
15. WALK B: facing RIGHT, headphones on, mid stride with his right leg forward, arms swung the other way.
16. THUMBS UP: facing LEFT, headphones on, one hand raised by his chest with a small deadpan thumbs up, the other arm relaxed, a tiny satisfied smile.

NEVER substitute. Do not add extra frames, merge two frames, repeat a pose, add any prop other than the headphones, add effects, or add the monster. If anything is unclear, draw the character in the described pose.
