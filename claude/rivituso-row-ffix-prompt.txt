- Lettering is bold, slightly rounded hand-painted capitals with even spacing, a thin light highlight on the top edge of the letters and a soft shadow under them, lit from the upper left. Strong contrast between letters and face.

ART DIRECTION
In the style of the hand-drawn watercolor and ink artwork of Final Fantasy IX: a lovingly illustrated location from a classic Japanese fantasy role-playing game, nostalgic, magical, charming, slightly exaggerated, full of personality.
- Traditional hand-painted watercolor and gouache look, visible organic brushwork, layered pigment, softly textured surfaces.
- Expressive dark brown and sepia contour lines on rooflines, windows, doors, timber frames, fences and silhouettes; stronger lines on major silhouettes, delicate ink on ornament.
- Chunky painterly shading in a few clear tonal groups, not smooth digital gradients.
- Rich warm colors, luminous golden highlights, soft lavender shadows, subtle watercolor color variation.
- Slightly imperfect hand-drawn geometry, ornate but readable detail, like a premium illustrated background.
Final Fantasy IX environmental proportions: translate the character design philosophy of large heads, compact bodies, sturdy shoes and expressive faces into the architecture: oversized expressive roofs, compact walls, substantial wooden foundations, large welcoming doors, slightly exaggerated windows. Chimneys may curve gently, roof ridges may bow, beams may be irregular, details may be playfully oversized. Each home should feel like a distinct character with its own personality, just as the party members in Final Fantasy IX have immediately recognizable silhouettes. Preserve the homes' established architectural identities while introducing fantasy-storybook exaggeration. The result should resemble a richly detailed, hand-painted background from a classic fantasy role-playing game.
Avoid photorealism, realistic architectural rendering, shiny plastic 3D, glossy mobile-game look, generic medieval buildings, extreme anime cel shading, pixel art and flat vector art.

SETTING
A cozy residential lane on Long Island, transformed through the romantic fairy-tale visual language of Final Fantasy IX, still recognizably a warm, lived-in Long Island family neighborhood, rendered with the charm, whimsy and handcrafted detail of a fantasy RPG village. Clear warm summer afternoon. Golden sunlight from the upper left, long soft shadows toward the lower right, lilac ambient light in the shade. Honey-cream cobblestone paths, slightly irregular and individually painted, wind between lush lawns, vegetable gardens, flower beds, timber fences and mature leafy trees; moss and small flowers grow only along path edges. Trees have rounded, expressive canopies with layered brushwork, dark ink accents and sunlight on leaf clusters. Lanterns, windows, chimneys, roof ornaments and garden details have the charming, handcrafted quality of a classic illustrated fantasy village. Everything feels welcoming, nostalgic, magical and unmistakably inhabited. Exactly three modest family homes. No mansions.

A player must be able to walk from either exit to every front door and completely around every house.
- Every house is freestanding, fully inside the picture, about one quarter of the image width, with a continuous cobblestone path on all four sides, including behind it.
- At least one full path width of cobblestones between every house and the image edge. No two buildings touch.
- No fence, deck, garden bed, tree or decoration blocks a path. Benches, lamps, bicycles, planters sit beside paths.
- Every front door connects to the main lane by a short cobbled approach. Paths behind each home rejoin the main lane on both sides.
- A main cobblestone lane loops through the center, connecting all three homes. Paths feel naturally curved and organically laid, as in a whimsical Final Fantasy IX town, but stay wide and unambiguous, readable even under roofs and canopies.
