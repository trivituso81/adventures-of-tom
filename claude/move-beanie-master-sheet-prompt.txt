One image, a grid of 3 columns by 2 rows of equal square cells, on a flat solid magenta #FF00FF background. The move: Beanie Master. She calls a very stinky fart a "beanie". It is a silly, harmless family joke: a funny cartoon stink cloud, a sickly green-yellow with soft swirls and a few wavy stink lines, painted solid and opaque so it never blends into the magenta background. No people or objects are damaged. The joke is in her face and body, not the cloud. The cloud is new in frame 3 and grows from there. It is small in frame 3 and fills the space behind her by frame 6. It is never pink, purple or magenta.

2. Stopped, both hands on her stomach, eyes wide, lips pressed tight. No cloud yet.
3. Turned away, glancing back over her shoulder, eyebrows raised, a guilty look. A small green-yellow stink puff has just appeared behind her.

6. Hands clasped at her chest, cheeks pink, a sheepish grin, shoulders raised. The cloud is large, soft and starting to thin out behind her.
