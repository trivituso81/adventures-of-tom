# emofull1 (2026-11-12 ~23:55 PT, main 6891d3d): mood poses show in full whichever way the person faces

- Tom 23:38: "Bodies in the maps and areas and rooms are not responding to emotional states the way that they were in the past... it doesn't matter which direction they're facing and the direction only matters for when they're not in an emotional state."
- Cause (fact): `emoFrontOk` in engine/emotions.js (added in pieface2 for three moods, widened to every mood in close1) drew the painted `em_*` pose only when the person faced the camera ('down'). cutstage1 and near1 turn everyone toward the speaker, so almost nobody faced the camera and no mood bodies appeared.
- Fix: `emoFrontOk` and both checks (speaker in `emoApply`, listeners in `emoReact`) removed. The drawing code (`scenePoseDraw`, engine/story.js) never filtered by facing, so nothing else changed. After the line the person returns to their normal stance in the direction they faced.
- Tested: `reaction_test.py` on the opening (ch1 town) and ch12Carmen in portrait. Mood poses drawn while facing up, left and right; frames open-10 and carmen-p-04 looked at. Smoke FAILS: []. Landscape not looked at; full scene audit not run.
- STANDING RULE (Tom 2026-11-12 23:38): a mood pose is shown in full whichever way the person faces; facing only decides the front, back, left, right stance when no mood is shown. Never gate a mood pose on facing again. This supersedes the next.md rule line "Mood poses (`em_*`) are front-facing only and drawn only when the person faces the camera" and the close1 note about `emoFrontOk`.
