SHEET 1 OF 9: CONTROLLER AND FINAL BOSS
============================================================

IMAGE REQUEST: ONE POSE SHEET. Return exactly one image, nothing else.

ATTACH: the reference picture of the character (or the character photo) so the look carries over.

WHAT THIS IS
Five extra action frames of the same character as the reference, for a family role-playing game. Draw the character EXACTLY like the reference in every frame: same face, clothes, colors, proportions, art style, level of detail and head size. Only the poses, actions and objects below are new. Do not redesign anything about the character.

THE SHEET (strict)
- One row of 5 equal cells, 400 x 500 pixels each, so the full image is 2000 x 500. If your tool offers a different size, keep exactly 5 equal cells in a single row.
- Exactly 5 frames, one per cell, left to right in the numbered order below.
- The character FACES LEFT in every frame (side or three-quarter view), unless a frame says it faces the viewer.
- Background: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF filling the whole image. No magenta anywhere on the character or objects. No magenta glow, spill or fringe. No grid lines, borders, cell backgrounds, numbers, labels, captions or titles.
- Each frame sits fully inside its cell with at least 10 percent margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or overlaps a neighbour.
- Same scale in every cell. Standing feet rest on one horizontal line about 30 pixels above the cell bottom. Ground poses rest on that same line.
- Draw ONLY the character plus the objects a frame names. Objects are drawn in the same painted style as the character and appear only when held by or touching the character. No other people, no text, no word balloons, no shadows, no dust, no motion lines, no sparkles, no glow effects.
- All objects are generic: no brand names, no logos, no readable letters.
- Each of the 5 frames must be clearly different from the others. Strong, readable, exaggerated silhouettes.

THE 5 FRAMES
1. HOLD: standing, both hands in front of the chest gripping a retro game controller (cream-gray body, plus-shaped D-pad on the left, one red and one mustard yellow button on the right), thumbs on the buttons, head tilted down at it, a tense focused squint.
2. ANNOYED: same stance, one arm stretched forward holding the same controller out at arm's length by its middle, the other hand on the hip, head tipped back, a flat deadpan stare of disbelief.
3. THROW WIND-UP: body twisted away from the left, throwing arm cocked far back with the same controller gripped like a baseball, weight on the back leg, eyes narrowed at a target to the left.
4. THROW FOLLOW-THROUGH: lunging far forward to the left, throwing arm at full extension with an empty open hand (the controller has just left it), back leg trailing, mouth open in a shout.
5. ARMS WIDE: feet planted wide, both arms flung out to the sides, hands empty, chest out, head thrown back laughing, a big triumphant boss-level cackle.

Family friendly for ages 6 and up.
