Draw the character in the attached pictures as one battle move sheet.

Picture 1 is the character. Picture 2 is their walk sheet. Pictures 3 and 4 are their battle sheets. Copy them as exactly as possible in every frame: the same person, face, age, body, head size compared to the body, hair, clothes, colors, painting style, level of detail, viewing angle and figure size as pictures 3 and 4. Only the pose and the objects in the hands change.

One image, a grid of 3 columns by 2 rows of equal square cells, on a flat solid magenta #FF00FF background. One figure per cell, the same size in every cell, feet on the same ground line, each figure well inside its cell. Held objects are painted in the same style as the character.

In every frame the character faces the same way as in pictures 3 and 4, in the same three-quarter view, unless a frame says she looks at the viewer.

The two plush lambs. They are the same size (each about as big as her head) and must look clearly different from each other in every frame they appear in:
- LAMBY is old and well loved: wool gone dingy grey-beige and matted flat in places, a faded square patch stitched on its tummy, one ear worn thin and droopy, one button eye a different color from the other, a faded pink bow tie that is loose and crooked.
- BACKUP LAMBY is new: bright clean white fluffy wool, both ears perky, two matching shiny black button eyes, a crisp bright pink bow tie.
LAMBY is always in her LEFT hand and BACKUP LAMBY is always in her RIGHT hand. Never swap them, never make them look alike, and the two lambs never merge into one shape.

The move: Lamby Boomerang.

Row 1:
1. Standing, LAMBY dangling from a pink hair ribbon in her left hand and BACKUP LAMBY dangling from a second pink hair ribbon in her right hand, LAMBY hanging limp and droopy, BACKUP LAMBY hanging fluffy and upright, a determined grin.
2. Whirling both lambs around on their ribbons over her head, both arms up, LAMBY's dingy grey-beige shape swinging on the left ribbon and BACKUP LAMBY's bright white shape swinging on the right ribbon, well apart so they never touch, mouth open in a whoop.
3. Letting go, both arms flung forward with open empty hands, front foot stepping out, eyes wide. No lambs and no ribbons in this frame.

Row 2:
4. Shading her eyes with one flat hand, watching far away, up on tiptoe, the other hand empty. No lambs and no ribbons in this frame.
5. Catching the lambs, arms out, both knees bent, laughing: LAMBY (dingy, patched, droopy ear) landing in her left hand and BACKUP LAMBY (bright white, perky ears) landing in her right hand, ribbons trailing.
6. Hugging both lambs to her chest, LAMBY's patched tummy and crooked bow tie against her left side, BACKUP LAMBY's bright pink bow tie against her right side, both faces visible, bouncing on her toes, a proud smile.
