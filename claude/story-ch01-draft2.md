## Scene 3. Deedee's kitchen
Idea: Deedee has guarded twelve muffins from Boppy all morning. A ball of mud takes all twelve in two seconds, and Boppy's only problem is that the mud got one before he did.
[Twelve apple muffins cool in a pan on the counter by the back screen door. Boppy sits at the table with an empty plate.]
- Deedee: There he is! Come here. Let me look at you. I missed that face. Now sit, you're having a muffin.
- Tom: Hi, Mom. I missed yours too. Is that the cinnamon topping? I've been thinking about that topping since we flew over Kansas.
- Deedee: Twelve of them, and all twelve are still there. I've been standing between that pan and your father since six this morning.
- Boppy: In my own kitchen! Tom, back at Rikers I had a key to every door in the place, and here I can't get near a muffin. You know, one time a fella tried to walk out of the mess hall with a whole loaf of bread under his hat...
[Boppy is still talking, with his back to the screen door.]
[BANG. The screen door flies open. A ball of mud rolls in, knocks Tom, Grace and Rose flat, grabs the muffin pan and rolls back out. Rose scrambles behind Tom.]
- Rose: AHHH! DEEDEE!
- Tom: GAH! I'm on the floor. Grace, is that your elbow or mine?
- Grace: It's mine! Tata, get UP, it's getting away!
- Deedee: That thing took my good twelve-cup pan! I don't even put that pan in the DISHWASHER.
- Boppy: ...and I only caught him because the hat kept getting taller. Where'd the muffins go?
- Deedee: A ball of mud came through the door and took them. Pan and all.
- Boppy: The mud gets a muffin and I don't?
- Deedee: Girls, sneakers. Tom, hold the door. Hubby, you stay right there. When we get them back, the first one's yours.
- Boppy: The first one. Tom, you heard her.
[Deedee, Tom and the girls run out the back door. Boppy sits back down with his empty plate.]

## Scene 4. The path into Grump Hollow
Idea: Rose asks the mud nicely, and can't believe that saying please didn't work.
[Three lumps of mud sit in the reeds, each holding muffins in both hands.]
- Rose: Hello, mud! Can we please have Deedee's muffins back? Thank you!
- Mudling: No. Ours now.
- Rose: But I said PLEASE!
- Tom: She did. I was right here. It was an excellent please.
- Deedee: Girls, behind me. You three have till I count to three. Then I'm getting the hose.
[The Mudlings push all the muffins into their mouths at once.]
- Deedee: Three.

- Grace: Nine papers. There were twelve. So the one with the pan still has three.
- Deedee: Then it has three of my muffins and my pan. Keep walking.

## Scene 5. Boppy's fishing spot
Idea: Rose wants the dragonfly rescued. Tom takes the job seriously and loses to something the size of a plum, while Grace runs the investigation.
[Beside Boppy's folding chair, a patch of grass the size of a hand is flat grey. On it sits an orange cube the size of a plum. A dragonfly is frozen inside it. One side of the cube has a small round dent.]
- Rose: The grass is Boppy color!
- Grace: It's grey, Rose. Just that one spot. And that orange cube has a dragonfly in it.
- Rose: He's stuck! Tata, get him out!
- Tom: Yes. Right away. Everybody give the dragonfly some room.
[Tom takes the cube in both hands and pulls. It does not move. He plants his feet and pulls again. Nothing.]
- Tom: It's the size of a plum. I carried a suitcase through two airports this morning.
- Rose: Your face is RED, Tata!
- Grace: Tata, stop. You're getting fingerprints on it. And look, it has a dent. Did you do that?
- Tom: Grace. I couldn't even make it wobble.
[Deedee bends down and picks something out of the grass with a tissue.]
- Grace: Deedee, what's that?

- Grace: Don't throw it out! It's a clue. I'm starting a new list. Burnt bulb. Grey grass. Orange cube with a dragonfly in it. I'm writing it in pencil.
- Tom: Why pencil?
- Grace: Because I don't know what any of it means yet.
[A huge splash from across the pond. Everyone turns.]

## Scene 6. The Big One
Idea: For twenty years Boppy has been underselling this fish. And the fish has been listening to Boppy the whole time.
[A bright green fish the size of a cab sits half out of the water. Deedee's muffin pan is on its head. Something gold shines in its lip.]
- Tom: GAH! Grace, I'm holding your hand. That's for me, not for you.
- Grace: I know, Tata. You're squeezing. Is that the Big One? Boppy only said it was as big as the couch!
- Deedee: For once in his life he rounded down.
- The Big One: GLUB. I was small at breakfast. Then a flash. Now I don't fit under my log.
- Deedee: And that's my good twelve-cup pan on your head. It's never been in pond water in its life.
- The Big One: It's my crown. A little muddy one brought it. I'm keeping it.
[Deedee starts down the bank toward the water.]
- Tom: Mom. That's a boss. You don't just walk up to a boss.
- Deedee: I worked the night shift, Tom. I've walked up to worse. Mister, take off my pan.
[Boss fight: the Big One.]
[The fish sulks in the shallows, still wearing the pan. Rose tiptoes through the reeds behind it.]
- Rose: BOO!
[The fish jumps. The pan flies off its head and lands on the bank. Then the fish laughs so hard the whole pond shakes.]
- The Big One: GLUB! HA! You got me! I see everybody. I never saw you!
- Tom: Nobody does. She's gotten me in an empty room.
[The fish pushes the pan across the water with its nose. Then it works a gold coin out of its lip and holds it out to Rose. A dragonfly is stamped on the coin.]
- The Big One: Take the pan. The sneaky one gets this. It's the only thing I've got.
- Rose: Thank you, fish! There's a bug on my money!
- The Big One: The man in the folding chair. Will he be back?
- Grace: That's Boppy. He's here every single day.
- The Big One: Good. He was in the middle of a story. The one about the race.
- Deedee: He's been in the middle of that one for fifty-one years.
[The fish sinks. Deedee picks up the pan. It is empty.]
