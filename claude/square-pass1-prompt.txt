LAYERING RULES (for cutting the painting into layers so a small figure can walk BEHIND tall objects)
1. Ground is one continuous layer. Every cobble, path, grass patch and street is fully painted behind and under every upright object. Where a tree trunk, lamp post, sign post, bench or the fountain stands, the ground around its base is visible on all sides.
2. Keep at least a 40 px strip of clearly visible walkable ground directly behind every tall object's base (trunks, lamp posts, sign posts).
3. Tree canopies may overhang the plaza edges by up to 60 px but must never cover a door, a sign, the fountain, an exit or the 64 px of cobble in front of a door. Every tree has a clear, tall trunk showing its base on the ground, and a canopy with a clean closed silhouette and a slightly darker rim, so foliage never blends into the ground.
4. Every upright object has a crisp, closed silhouette with strong value or color separation from what is behind it. No fuzzy edges, no glow bleeding onto neighbors, no haze, no depth blur.
5. Keep objects separate: at least 24 px of clear space between any two upright objects (a lamp never touches a tree canopy; the fountain touches nothing; signs do not touch each other).
6. Each building reads as clear layers: a hard horizontal base line where the front wall meets the ground; the wall with its door and windows; an eave or awning band; the roof on top. Chimneys, emblems and finials stand clear of each other. Awnings and eaves cast only a small soft shadow on the wall, never a dark shadow across a door or path.
7. Shadows on the ground are soft, short and low contrast, falling to the lower right, so the ground stays clean when an object is cut away. No dark baked shadows across paths or doors. No dark corners.
8. Storefront awnings and canopies have open ground visible beneath them and nothing hanging below their lower edge.
9. About 12 trees in total: dense rows along the top, left and right edges and below the south row, plus three medium trees standing alone on the plaza at about (90, 700), (1810, 790) and (1400, 560). Each stand-alone tree has at least 100 px of clear ground on each side and a canopy no wider than 220 px.
