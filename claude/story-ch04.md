What happens: Tom flies back to Los Angeles to watch TNB work on a big movie shoot. TNB meets him at the cab stand and hangs a visitor lanyard around his neck, which is how TNB keeps Deedee's rule that nobody goes anywhere alone. They pick up Nara and go to Sunset Gold Studios. Rack two, with forty steamed costumes on it, has vanished from Soundstage 7 after a huge flash, and the fake streets of the Backlot are full of things that woke up: a crab made of cue cards, a clapperboard that snaps, a moth in a boom microphone. Tom, TNB and Nara clear the three sets and get into Soundstage 7. When TNB gets the lights back on, there is a circle of floor with no color, and on TNB's wardrobe table the cowboy hat for the whole movie is frozen on its hat stand inside a hard orange block as big as a beach ball. TNB slides the table out from under it and the hat stays hanging in the air. Up on the stage set a megaphone as big as a refrigerator, wearing a beret, is sitting on the rack. It woke up after the flash and wears a gold coin on its beret as its first award. They tell it "great take", and it gives the award to TNB for best costumes. The rack goes back, the shoot is saved with a backup hat, and the hat in the block stays frozen.

What the family knows at the end: it has now happened four times, twice on each coast. Tom knows the signs so well that he finds the round hole and the burnt flashbulb before TNB has finished being shocked. TNB has seen a block hang in the air with his own eyes and has a coin of his own. Grace has the hat on page three of her notebook. Nobody has a theory.

### The fight
