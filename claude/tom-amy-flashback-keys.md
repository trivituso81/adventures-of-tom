# Tom + Amy flashback Tombos: image prompts and frame keys (2026-10-31)

Two flashback Tombos use child Tom and child Amy (family-lab-feedback.md cross-cutting rule): Ymamot Creation (Tombo 2) and Knock It Off! (Tombo 3). One 4x4 sheet each (1024 x 1024, 256 cells): Tom frames 1 to 8 in rows 1 and 2, Amy frames 1 to 8 in rows 3 and 4. Tom attaches the kid Tom walk sheet and the kid Amy hero image to each. Prompts: ymamot-creation-prompt.txt, knock-it-off-prompt.txt. Not other Tom + Amy moves: I Called It!, Opening Night, Party Decorators, Elevator Cheat are adult scenes.

## Keys, Ymamot Creation (ym_)

Lines: Amy "Ketchup, pickles, cereal, one sock. It's a Ymamot Creation!" / Tom "Yama-Mott. It's Tom backwards. We were six."
Game draws: bowl, ingredients, food splatter, kitchen. Sheet has only the spoon, ketchup bottle, cereal box, pickle, striped sock.
