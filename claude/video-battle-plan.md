## 1. The rule (Tom's words, also in CLAUDE.md and claude/canon-addendum.md)

NO PAINTED POSES IN BATTLE. No hand-painted or image-generated still frames, poses or sprite sheets for any party member or monster in any battle state. Battle animation comes from video generated by a video model (WAN or equivalent), including idle breathing. Overworld and story scenes never get breathing.

| What the rule means for a session | |
|---|---|
| Never | Build, tune, review or propose a painted pose, a still frame moved by code, a cutout rig or a sprite sheet for a battle state. Not as a fallback, not as a stand-in, not after a video misses |
| Allowed | A still picture as the FIRST or LAST FRAME handed to the video model (the start picture). It is an input and is never drawn in a fight |
| When a video misses | Another video: a changed prompt, start picture, setting or model, by the smallest change. Two misses the same way is the stop-loss |
| Idle | The idle is a video loop with the breathing in it. No size pulse in code |

**Call 2, the video.** fal endpoint `alibaba/wan-3.0/image-to-video`.

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `start_image_url` | the start picture | |
| `end_image_url` | the same picture | The clip ends in the standing stance it began in, as the Sorceress clips did |
| `resolution` | 720p | The size of the clips Tom liked |
| `aspect_ratio` | 9:16 | Upright, so the figure fills the frame |
| `duration` | 5 | As the clips Tom liked (the model's shortest is 2) |
| `audio` | false | Silent |
| `enable_prompt_expansion` | true (the default) | Left as the host sets it. It is the one dial for a second try if the model adds anything |
| `seed` | 81 | Fixed, so a run can be repeated |

The prompt, exactly. It is `BOOP_APPROVED` (tools/art-library/clip-prompts.js) with only the MOTION part changed, per the repo rule for a person clip:

```
IMAGE TO VIDEO REQUEST: ONE SHORT ANIMATION CLIP.

WHAT THIS IS
A 2D sprite animation of the exact character in the attached first-frame image, preserving her exact face, proportions, hair, outfit, accessories, and hand-drawn colored pencil and ink caricature style throughout every frame.

CAMERA AND BACKGROUND
Locked static camera with zero movement. The character stays facing left with her feet planted on the exact same spot on a flat, solid pure magenta background with no shadows, lighting changes, or visual effects. Silent clip.

MOTION: BRACED GUARD
From her starting relaxed stance, she bends her knees, tucks her chin and raises both arms once, crossing her forearms in an X in front of her face with both fists closed. She holds that braced guard, breathing gently, then lowers her arms and stands back up into her starting stance.
```

What was kept and what was changed, against the guard prompt that gave praying hands ("raises both forearms in front of her face and chest"): kept the three-part shape and every word outside MOTION. Changed in MOTION: the forearms are crossed in an X, the fists are closed, and the clip returns to the stance.
