## The re-anchor (decided, pending Tom's OK)
- The man in the long coat IS Julian Vance, the Curator. He collects things nobody came back for, because he was the one who never came back (missed his daughter Clara's ninth birthday, his mom's last days). The coins were his anchors. He needed Tom, the one person who flies between the last two moving places, to open Gate Zero for him. The family's kindness did it. "Thank you for flying nowhere" is a real thank-you.
- The rest of the world is already paused (bible twist, kept): every other town is stuck in its happiest afternoon, like a photo. Long Island and Los Angeles kept moving because Tom keeps flying between them.
- Boppy's betrayal moves INTO Part 2: the seed is ch11 (a gold card in his recliner, "junk mail"), the reveal is ch13's last scene, the fight is ch14. Same fear as the bible (getting slower while the girls get faster), same redemption.
- New recurring villains are introduced in Part 2: HUSH-9 (ch13, a quiet-machine that hates noisy families; final form ch15) and Monochrome Marlowe (ch12, Vance's fixer who greys out places; transformed in ch16). Orton is dropped (the Last Call took his slot).
