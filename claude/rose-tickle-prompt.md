# Rose Tickle Storm: 16-frame sheet prompt (v2, 2026-10-30)

Replaces the 8-frame prompt (tk_creep, tk_pounce, tk_tickleA/B/C, tk_feather, tk_finale, tk_doubled). Tom attaches a reference image of painted Rose with the prompt.

Frame keys in order (left to right, top to bottom): tk_ready, tk_creepA, tk_creepB, tk_launch, tk_air, tk_land, tk_tickleA, tk_tickleB, tk_tickleC, tk_tickleD, tk_feather_draw, tk_feather_swish, tk_feather_twirl, tk_finale, tk_doubled, tk_proud.

## Prompt text

IMAGE REQUEST: ONE SINGLE CHARACTER ANIMATION SHEET. Return exactly one image, nothing else.

WHAT THIS IS
Animation frames for "The Adventures of Tom", a family role-playing game in the style of a 16-bit Super Nintendo game (Final Fantasy VI, Chrono Trigger). The character is Rose, a 6-year-old girl. The attached reference image shows Rose. Draw her EXACTLY like the reference in every frame: same face, hair, outfit, colors, proportions, art style and level of detail.

THE MOVE
"Tickle Storm": Rose sneaks up on a monster, pounces on it, tickles it in a furious blur, whips out a rainbow feather and tickles it with the feather, then finishes with a giggling victory. Silly, joyful, mischievous. Big grin, sparkling eyes and bouncy energy in every frame. Friendly and funny, never mean.

THE SHEET (strict)
- A grid of 4 columns by 4 rows of square cells, 256 x 256 pixels each, so the full image is 1024 x 1024. If your tool offers a different size, keep exactly 4 columns and 4 rows.
- Exactly 16 frames, one per cell, filled left to right, top to bottom, in the order of the numbered list below.
- Same character at the same scale in every cell. Standing Rose is about 170 pixels tall. She gets shorter when crouched and wider when lunging, but never rescale her per frame.
- Every frame sits fully inside its cell with at least 10 percent empty margin on every side. Nothing touches or crosses a cell edge or overlaps a neighbouring cell.
- In every frame where her feet are on the ground, the soles rest on the same horizontal line, about 28 pixels above the bottom of the cell.
- Rose faces LEFT (toward the viewer's left) in every frame, in a side or three-quarter view from slightly above, like a party member in a 16-bit battle scene facing an enemy off to the left.
- Background: one flat solid color, pure magenta #FF00FF, filling the whole image. No magenta anywhere in Rose. No grid lines, borders, cell backgrounds, numbers, labels, captions or titles.
- Draw ONLY Rose. No ground shadow, dust, speed lines, sparkles, stars, motion blur or any effect floating outside her body (effects are added in the game, so show motion through her pose alone). No monster, no other people, no stuffed lamb, no toys.
- The only object allowed is the rainbow feather, and only in frames 11, 12 and 13: one long fluffy feather shading from red through orange, yellow, green and blue to violet.

ART STYLE
- 32-bit era high-detail pixel art (late SNES, Saturn, PlayStation quality): rich shading, 24 to 32 colors, clean readable silhouette, no anti-aliasing blur, no outline thicker than one pixel.
- A crisp one-pixel darker rim around her outer edge and a faint lighter highlight along her top edge.
- Soft key light from the upper left, shadow on the lower right, warm slightly desaturated painterly palette. All 16 frames share one look and one lighting direction.
- Family friendly for children aged 6 and up.

THE 16 FRAMES (each must look clearly different from the others)
1. READY: standing, knees slightly bent, both hands raised in front of her chest with fingers spread and wiggling, a sly sideways grin, eyebrows up. Plotting.
2. CREEP A: tiptoeing toward the left, left foot lifted high, body leaning forward, hands up by her shoulders with fingers spread, mouth pressed shut to hold in a giggle, eyes wide and sparkling.
3. CREEP B: the opposite tiptoe step, right foot lifted, body low and sneaky, one finger of one hand pressed to her lips (shhh), cheeks puffed with a held-in giggle.
4. LAUNCH: deep crouch coiled like a spring, knees bent hard, arms swung back behind her, eyes locked forward, a determined open-mouthed grin.
5. AIR: mid-pounce, fully airborne, body stretched forward and slightly upward, both arms reaching far to the left with fingers spread like little claws, hair streaming back, mouth wide open in a battle yell. Feet off the ground and trailing.
6. LAND: landing low and close, knees bent, leaning far forward, both hands out to the left at chest height with fingers spread, huge gleeful grin. The moment before tickling.
7. TICKLE A: planted and leaning in, both hands out to the left at chest height, fingers wildly spread and wiggling, elbows bent, head tilted left, eyes squeezed into happy crescents, laughing mouth.
8. TICKLE B: same position but body tilted the other way, hands a little higher at shoulder height, one hand slightly ahead of the other, fingers blurred and wiggling, head tilted right, mouth wide in a giggle.
9. TICKLE C: one hand raised high and one low, as if tickling under the chin and the belly at once, torso twisted, one foot lifted off the ground, eyes open wide and delighted, tongue showing.
10. TICKLE D: the fastest, most frantic tickle: body hunched forward, both hands low and out to the left, arms bent tight, fingers spread, shoulders shaking, hair bouncing, eyes shut tight, huge open laughing mouth.
11. FEATHER DRAW: standing tall, one arm swept behind her back pulling the rainbow feather out, the other hand raised like a magician's reveal, a smug triumphant smirk, eyebrows raised.
12. FEATHER SWISH: leaning far forward to the left, the feather held in her right hand at arm's length, sweeping low across in front of her, the other arm out for balance, wide delighted grin.
13. FEATHER TWIRL: spinning, up on one toe with the other leg kicked out, the feather held high overhead in one hand, free arm out wide, hair and clothes swirling, head back, joyful open-mouthed laugh.
14. FINALE: feet planted wide, both arms thrown up and out in a big "ta-da", chest out, chin up, eyes closed in a huge happy laugh. The feather is gone and both hands are empty.
15. DOUBLED OVER: bent forward at the waist laughing, both hands on her knees, eyes squeezed shut, mouth wide open, cheeks pink, shoulders shaking, one foot slightly lifted.
16. PROUD: standing tall and straight, hands on her hips, chin lifted, a self-satisfied giggly grin, one eye winking.

NEVER substitute. Do not add extra frames, merge two frames, repeat a pose, or invent props. If anything is unclear, draw Rose in the described pose.
