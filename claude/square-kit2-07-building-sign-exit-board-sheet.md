## Sign rules (all eight)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Blend | Same materials, colors and age as its building; reads as built with the house, not stuck on |
| Placement | Flat on the front wall, centered above the door, about 3 percent of picture height of bare wall below it |
| Never touches | Door, awning, roof landmark, lantern |
| Size | At least 55 percent of the building width, bold caps, two lines |
| Finish | Hand-painted or carved, lightly weathered, never a clean flat plaque |
