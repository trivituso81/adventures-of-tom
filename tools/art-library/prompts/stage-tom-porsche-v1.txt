ONE PICTURE OF ONE OBJECT FOR A GAME.

A sleek black luxury SUV with tinted windows and polished silver wheels, its headlights switched on. Drawn in a hand-drawn colored pencil and ink illustration style with soft shading and warm golden light from the upper left, no thick outlines, not pixel art.

Seen exactly from the side, the front of the car facing left, both wheels on one level line. The whole object is centered with a wide empty margin on every side. Flat, solid pure magenta background (#FF00FF) with no shadow, no floor and nothing else in the picture.

No people, no hands, no logos or badges, no other text: the only lettering is a small white card propped inside the rear side window, which reads TOM in black capitals.
