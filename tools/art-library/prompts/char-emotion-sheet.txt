Draw the character in the attached picture as an emotion sheet: the same person showing eight different feelings.

The attached picture is the character. Copy it as exactly as possible in every frame: the same person, face, age, body, head size compared to the body, hair, clothes, colors, painting style and level of detail. Only the pose and the face change.

One image, a grid of 4 columns by 2 rows of equal square cells, on a flat solid magenta #FF00FF background. One full-body figure per cell, from the top of the hair to the feet, standing, facing the viewer in a slight three-quarter view. The same figure size in every cell, feet on the same ground line, each figure well inside its cell. Empty hands. No grid lines, numbers, text, symbols, tears, sweat drops, hearts or effects. Each feeling must read from the whole body and from the face, clear enough to read at a small size.

Row 1:
1. Happy. Up on the toes, knees loose, a little bounce. Both hands clasped at chest height. Eyes crinkled, cheeks raised, a big open-mouth smile.
2. Loving. Feet together, head tilted to one side. Both hands pressed flat over the heart. Soft half-closed eyes, a warm closed-mouth smile.
3. Sad. Slumped, shoulders dropped, head lowered, feet together with the toes turned in. Hands holding each other in front of the waist. Eyebrows pulled up in the middle, the corners of the mouth turned down.
4. Angry. Feet planted wide, leaning forward, arms crossed tight over the chest. Chin down, eyebrows pulled down hard, eyes narrowed, a tight frown.

Row 2:
5. Scared. Knees bent, leaning back, shoulders hunched up. Both fists held up under the chin. Eyes wide open, eyebrows raised and pulled together, mouth open and stretched wide.
6. Surprised. One foot stepped back, upper body pulled back. Both hands open beside the face, palms forward. Eyebrows raised high, eyes wide and round, mouth open in a tall O.
7. Proud. Feet apart, chest out, shoulders back, chin raised. Both fists on the hips. A confident closed-mouth one-sided smile.
8. Unimpressed. Weight on one leg, the other knee relaxed. One hand on the hip, the other arm hanging loose. Head tilted, eyelids half lowered, one eyebrow raised, mouth a flat line pulled to one side.
