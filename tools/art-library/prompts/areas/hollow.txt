SETTING
A soggy, slightly spooky but friendly woodland hollow at the edge of a small Long Island town, on a warm afternoon. Golden sunlight from the upper left breaks through the leafy canopy in soft shafts, with long dappled shadows falling to the lower right and faint lilac light in the shade. A big murky green pond with lily pads and cattails, mossy logs, old stumps, mud puddles, ferns, mushrooms and mossy boulders. Cozy and mischievous, never scary: a place where small mud creatures might live.

THIS IS A WALKABLE GAME MAP: NAVIGATION COMES FIRST
A character must be able to walk from the entrance to every landmark. No tree, log, rock or prop may wall off a route. Every path is at least two character-widths wide.
- ENTRANCE: a dirt trail enters from the BOTTOM edge, a little left of the center, through a gap in a low wooden fence.
- The trail climbs straight up into a WIDE OPEN MUDDY CLEARING in the middle of the picture, big and flat, with room to walk around freely.
- From the clearing a branch runs LEFT to a soggy bench nook on the left side, and another branch runs RIGHT to Boppy's fishing spot on the pond shore.
- A narrower trail continues from the clearing UP the left side to the pond's upper left shore.
- THE POND fills the upper right and top center, about four tenths of the picture width. Its lower and left shores are walkable mud; no path goes into the water.
- No other exits: the left, right and top edges are closed by thick trees, boulders and brambles.
- Logs, stumps, rocks, ferns and cattails sit beside the paths, never across them.

LANDMARKS AND PROPS
- Soggy bench nook (left side): an old wooden bench sinking a little into the mud, puddles around its feet, moss on the slats.
- Boppy's fishing spot (right side, on the pond shore): a small weathered wooden bench with a thermos, a metal bucket and a fishing rod leaning on it, facing the water.
- Around the pond: lily pads, cattails, a half-sunken mossy log in the water, ripples, a few water lilies.
- Mossy boulders, old stumps, a fallen log beside the left trail, clumps of mushrooms, ferns, wildflowers.
