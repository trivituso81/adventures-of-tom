LIKENESS AND STYLE
- Draw this character EXACTLY like picture 1 in every frame: same face, hair, clothes, prints, colors, shoes, proportions and head size, in the same painting style and level of detail.
- Keep the face as faithful to picture 2 as picture 1 is, in every frame.
- Painted and realistic, like picture 1. Not pixel art, not 16-bit, not a cartoon, not a 3D render.

THE SHEET
- 4 columns by 3 rows of equal square cells (about 1024 x 768 in all), filled left to right, top to bottom, in the order below. Exactly 12 frames, one per cell.
- Background: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF over the whole image. No magenta in the character and no magenta glow or fringe on hair or edges. No grid lines, borders, numbers, labels or text.
- Each figure sits fully inside its cell with at least 10 percent margin; nothing touches a cell edge or a neighbour.
- Same scale in every cell. Feet on the ground rest on one horizontal line near the bottom of each cell. The head is the same size in every frame.
- The character FACES LEFT in every frame, toward an opponent off to the left: a three-quarter view from the side, camera at chest height, full body.
- Only the character. Hands empty. No shadows, effects, sparkles, motion lines, props or other people (the game adds effects).

THE 12 FRAMES
1. READY: battle stance, feet shoulder width apart, knees slightly bent, hands loose, calm and alert.
2. RUN A: running to the left, near leg forward, opposite arm swinging forward.
3. RUN B: running to the left, the opposite step.
4. ACT: one arm thrust straight out to the left, hand in a fist, front foot forward, a determined look.
5. WIND UP: body twisted away from the left, one arm drawn back, weight on the back leg, eyes narrowed.
6. LEAP: jumping up and to the left, both feet off the ground, knees tucked, arms up.
7. CAST: both hands raised in front of the chest, palms open toward the left, focused, sending out a special move.
8. DEFEND: braced stance, one arm raised in front with the palm out in a calm "stop" gesture, the other arm close to the body, firm face.
9. FLINCH: rocked back and leaning right, eyes squeezed shut, a wince, one foot lifted.
10. TIRED: down on one knee, one hand on the raised knee, head low, out of breath.
11. NAP: sitting slumped on the ground, legs folded, head drooping, eyes closed, dizzy.
12. CHEER: victory, one fist raised high, the other hand on the hip, a big grin.
