How to do this

1. Paste your API key. 2. Deedee is open: press Design 3 options, play all three, press Use this voice on the warmest one. None right? Press it again for 3 more. 3. Click Aunt Ellie and do the same: pick the one that sounds most Long Island and least British. 4. Press Generate all. 5. Listen. Redo any line that missed, then Save recast.json and send it to Claude.

Directions in [square brackets] tell the voice how to act. Edit one if a line misses. Every bracket also carries the accent, which keeps it from fading mid-line.

Voice slots: if your account is full, saving a new voice removes the old Deedee or Ellie voice for you. Lines already in the game keep playing.
